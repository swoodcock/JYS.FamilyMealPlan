Beef Hot Dogs + Tossed Salad
Sunday dinner → Monday lunch · serves 4 + lunchbox
▾
Allergen-safeLow-carb swapHidden veg
Allergen-safe: Check hot dogs & buns are egg-free (most all-beef dogs are). Dijon mustard is typically egg-free — check the label. No nuts or peas anywhere.

Ingredients

  • 8 all-beef hot dogs (~450 g)
  • 8 soft hot dog buns · check egg-wash-free
  • 200 g mixed lettuce · 150 g cucumber, sliced · 150 g cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 100 g grated carrot · 60 g baby spinach, finely chopped hidden veg
  • Dressing: 30 ml olive oil, 15 ml red wine vinegar, 1 tsp Dijon mustard, pinch sugar
  • Vitamin-C side: orange segments or bell pepper strips vit C
  • Adults: hot sauce, spicy brown mustard, pickled jalapeños

Method

  1. Grill or pan-sear hot dogs until browned all over, 6–8 min.
  2. Toss lettuce, cucumber, tomatoes, carrot and spinach together. Keep the kids' portion undressed.
  3. Warm the buns, assemble, and serve the salad + vitamin-C side alongside.
🔄 Low-carb: skip the bun and wrap the hot dog in a lettuce leaf instead; the salad is already low-carb.
🩸 Lighter iron night: spinach in the salad plus a vitamin-C orange side helps make the most of it — the week's bigger iron nights follow later on.
🥡 Lunchbox: pack sliced hot dog rounds + salad separately to match Monday's daycare tray.

Spaghetti & Meat Sauce
Monday dinner → Tuesday lunch · serves 4 + lunchbox
▾
Allergen-safeIron-rich ★Hidden vegLow-carb swapAdult spice
Allergen-safe: Most dried spaghetti is just semolina + water (egg-free) — check the label. Sauce is naturally dairy/egg-free; parmesan on top is cooked/aged (fine), or leave it off.

Ingredients

  • 500 g spaghetti check egg-free
  • 500 g ground beef iron
  • 1 onion, diced · 2 garlic cloves · 1 carrot, grated · 1 zucchini, grated hidden veg
  • 60 g baby spinach, chopped iron
  • 700 g crushed tomatoes · 30 g tomato paste vit C
  • 1 tsp dried oregano, 1 tsp dried basil
  • Parmesan to serve (optional) · Adults: chili flakes

Method

  1. Brown beef with onion and garlic. Stir in carrot, zucchini and spinach; cook 3–4 min.
  2. Add crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, oregano and basil. Simmer 20 min.
  3. Boil spaghetti per packet instructions; toss with the sauce or serve sauce on top.
🔄 Low-carb: serve the sauce over zucchini noodles or spaghetti squash instead of pasta.
🩸 Iron-rich ★: beef + spinach + tomato (vitamin C) — a strong iron night.
🌶️ Adults: chili flakes stirred in at the table.
🥡 Lunchbox: pack spaghetti + sauce together, veg on the side, to match Tuesday's daycare tray.

Premade Short Rib Stew + Mash
Tuesday dinner → Wednesday lunch · serves 4 + lunchbox
▾
Allergen-safe (check label)Iron-richLow-carb swap
Allergen-safe: This is the one dish this week we don't cook from scratch — check the premade/frozen stew's label for egg, dairy, tree nut, peanut, or pea content before serving.

Ingredients

  • ~1–1.2 kg premade/frozen short rib stew iron
  • Mash: 900 g potatoes + butter + milk — plus half a cauliflower head (~300 g) for the low-carb mash
  • Vitamin-C side: orange wedges vit C

Method

  1. Thaw the stew in the fridge if frozen (from Sunday). Reheat gently on the stovetop or in the oven, 15–20 min, stirring occasionally, until piping hot throughout.
  2. Boil potatoes (and the cauliflower half separately); mash each with butter + milk.
  3. Serve the stew over or alongside the mash, with orange wedges on the side.
🔄 Low-carb: cauliflower mash instead of potato.
🩸 Iron-rich: short rib is a strong iron source — pair with the vitamin-C orange wedges to help absorption.
🥡 Lunchbox: stew + mash reheats well. The mashed-potato side still matches Wednesday's daycare tray, even though the protein is swapped this week.

Pancit Bihon
Wednesday dinner → Thursday lunch · serves 4 + lunchbox
▾
Allergen-safeIron-richHidden vegLow-carb swapAdult spice
Allergen-safe: Naturally egg-free — skip any fried-egg garnish. No nuts or peas; green beans stand in for peas.

Ingredients

  • 250 g rice vermicelli (bihon) noodles
  • 400 g chicken thigh, thinly sliced · optional 200 g beef strips extra iron
  • 1 onion, sliced · 2 garlic cloves · 200 g cabbage, shredded · 2 carrots, julienned · 100 g green beans, sliced no peas
  • 60 g baby spinach, chopped iron
  • 750 ml low-sodium chicken broth · 45 ml soy sauce · juice of 2 calamansi or 1 lemon vit C
  • Adults: chili garlic oil or fish sauce (patis)

Method

  1. Soak the bihon noodles in warm water until pliable; drain.
  2. Sauté onion and garlic; add chicken (and beef if using) and cook through.
  3. Add cabbage, carrot and green beans; stir-fry 3–4 min. Add spinach.
  4. Pour in broth and soy sauce, add the noodles, and toss until the liquid is absorbed, 5–6 min.
  5. Finish with a squeeze of calamansi or lemon.
🔄 Low-carb: swap the bihon for shirataki noodles or extra cabbage and bean sprouts.
🩸 Iron-rich: chicken (plus beef strips if used) and spinach, paired with calamansi/lemon vitamin C.
🌶️ Adults: chili garlic oil or a splash of fish sauce at the table.
🥡 Lunchbox: reheats well, matching Thursday's daycare tray.
5
Egg-Free Baked Chicken Fingers + Rice
Thursday dinner · serves 4 (no lunchbox needed this week)
▾
Allergen-safeLow-carb swapHidden vegAdult spice
Allergen-safe: A milk-soak replaces the usual raw egg wash — no egg anywhere. Check the breadcrumbs are egg-free.

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken breast, sliced into strips
  • 80 ml milk, for soaking
  • 100 g breadcrumbs (egg-free) · 30 g grated parmesan · 1 tsp paprika, salt, pepper
  • Rice for the family · 1 cauliflower head, for the low-carb "rice"
  • Carrots + 200 g green beans no peas · extra finely diced carrot/zucchini stirred into the rice hidden veg
  • Adults: hot honey or sriracha mayo

Method

  1. Soak chicken strips in milk for 10 min (tenderizes without needing egg). Mix breadcrumbs, parmesan and paprika.
  2. Dredge strips in the breadcrumb mix, pressing to coat. Place on a lined tray.
  3. Bake at 200°C (400°F) 15–18 min, flipping once, until golden and cooked through (74°C/165°F).
  4. Boil rice (or pulse cauliflower to rice-sized pieces and sauté for the low-carb version); stir in finely diced carrot/zucchini. Steam carrots + green beans.
🔄 Low-carb: cauliflower rice instead of rice; skip the breading on part of the chicken for a plain grilled low-carb strip.
🥦 Hidden veg: finely diced carrot and zucchini stirred through the rice.
🌶️ Adults: hot honey or sriracha mayo for dipping.
🥡 No lunchbox needed: we're traveling Thursday night, so there's no Friday lunch to pack this week — enjoy the extras as leftovers, or pack a portion as a road-trip snack.
