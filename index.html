Chicken Fingers + Rice
Sunday dinner → Monday lunch · 2 kid dinners + 2 kid lunches + 2 parent dinners
▾
Allergen-safe (check box)Iron boostHidden vegLow-carb swapFamily
Allergen-safe: Many frozen breaded chicken strips use egg in the batter or breading. Check the box for egg and for a nut warning. Milk in the breading is fine once baked.

Ingredients

  • Frozen premade chicken strips, egg-free — enough for 6 portions (about 700 g) iron
  • 220 g rice (dry), with 40 g carrot and ¼ zucchini, finely diced, cooked in hidden veg
  • 250 g broccoli, steamed vit C
  • Orange wedges, to serve vit C

Method

  1. Bake the strips as the box says, on two trays if needed.
  2. Meanwhile, cook the rice with the diced carrot and zucchini stirred in. Steam the broccoli.
🔄 Low-carb: skip the rice and double up on broccoli.
🩸 Iron boost: chicken, with broccoli and orange for vitamin C.
🥡 Lunchbox: they pack and reheat well. Cut the strips into small pieces for the younger one. Matches Monday's updated daycare lunch (Chicken fingers, Rice, Fruit, Milk).

Chicken, Broccoli & Rice Bake
Monday dinner → Tuesday lunch · 2 kid dinners + 2 kid lunches
▾
Allergen-safe (cooked milk)Hidden vegKid portions
Allergen-safe: The creamy sauce is milk cooked into a roux, with no cheese and no canned cream-of soup (some contain egg). Check the breadcrumbs are egg-free.

Ingredients

  • 300 g boneless chicken thighs, in small pieces iron
  • 120 g rice (dry)
  • 200 g broccoli, in small florets vit C · 60 g carrot, grated hidden veg
  • ½ onion + 1 clove garlic, finely chopped
  • Sauce: 20 g butter + 20 g flour + 250 ml milk + 300 ml chicken broth
  • Topping: 20 g breadcrumbs, egg-free

Method

  1. Cook the rice. Preheat the oven to 190°C (375°F).
  2. Brown the chicken in a little oil, 5 min. Add the onion, garlic and carrot and soften 3 min. Tip into a small baking dish.
  3. Melt the butter in the same pan, stir in the flour for 1 min, then whisk in the milk and broth. Simmer until it thickens, 3–4 min.
  4. Stir the rice, raw broccoli and sauce into the chicken. Top with the breadcrumbs and bake 25 min, until bubbling.
👶 Little ones: chop the chicken and broccoli small for the younger one.
🩸 Lighter iron night: chicken with broccoli for vitamin C. The lasagna follows with beef and spinach.
🥡 Lunchbox: reheats well with a splash of milk or water. Matches Tuesday's daycare tray (Chicken Broccoli Rice Casserole, Fruit, Milk).

Beef Lasagna
Tuesday dinner → Wednesday lunch · 2 kid dinners + 2 kid lunches + 2 parent dinners
▾
Allergen-safe (baked dairy)Iron-rich ★Hidden vegLow-carb swapFamily
Allergen-safe: A white sauce stands in for the usual ricotta-and-egg layer. All the milk and cheese is baked. Check the noodles are plain dried sheets, not egg pasta. The kids don't like cheese, so leave the mozzarella off one end of the dish for their portions.

Ingredients

  • 500 g ground beef iron ★
  • 1 onion + 3 cloves garlic, finely chopped · 100 g carrot + ½ zucchini, grated hidden veg
  • 2 small cans (398 ml) crushed tomatoes + 30 g tomato paste + 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 60 g baby spinach, chopped iron
  • 250 g lasagna noodles, egg-free (about 12 sheets)
  • White sauce: 40 g butter + 40 g flour + 600 ml milk + a pinch of nutmeg
  • 150 g shredded mozzarella, for the parents' end
  • Lunchbox & side veggies: 1 red bell pepper + ½ cucumber, as sticks vit C

Method

  1. Meat sauce (can be made Monday while the rice bake is in the oven): brown the beef, add the onion, garlic, carrot and zucchini and cook 5 min. Stir in the tomatoes, paste and oregano and simmer 15 min. Stir in the spinach to wilt.
  2. White sauce: melt the butter, stir in the flour for 1 min, then whisk in the milk gradually. Simmer until thick, 5 min. Season with salt and nutmeg.
  3. Preheat the oven to 190°C (375°F). In a 23 × 33 cm dish, layer sauce, noodles, meat sauce and white sauce three times, finishing with white sauce.
  4. Scatter the mozzarella over two-thirds of the top, leaving the kids' end bare. Cover with foil and bake 30 min, then uncover for 15 min until golden. Rest 10 min before cutting.
🔄 Low-carb: a small square of lasagna with a big side of the pepper and cucumber sticks or a green salad. Or bake a parent portion in a small dish with zucchini slices in place of the noodles.
🩸 Iron-rich ★: beef and spinach, with tomato and red pepper for vitamin C.
🥡 Lunchbox: cut 2 squares from the cheese-free end once cooled, with pepper sticks alongside. Matches Wednesday's daycare tray (Lasagna, Fruit, Milk).

Chicken & Bean Quesadillas
Wednesday dinner → Thursday lunch · 2 kid dinners + 2 kid lunches
▾
Allergen-safe (check tortillas)Iron-richHidden vegKid portions
Allergen-safe: No cheese: mashed black beans hold the filling together instead. Check the tortillas are egg-free.

Ingredients

  • 4 large flour tortillas, egg-free
  • 250 g chicken breast, in small pieces
  • 1 can black beans, drained and rinsed iron
  • 60 g frozen corn · ½ red bell pepper, finely diced vit C · 20 g baby spinach, finely chopped hidden veg
  • ½ tsp mild cumin + a pinch of smoked paprika
  • Raw veggies: ½ cucumber + 60 g carrot, as sticks

Method

  1. Cook the chicken in a little oil with the cumin and paprika, 6–7 min. Add the corn, pepper and spinach for the last 2 min.
  2. Mash half the beans with a fork and stir in the rest with the chicken mixture.
  3. Spread the filling over half of each tortilla and fold over. Toast in a dry pan over medium heat, 2–3 min a side, pressing down, until golden and crisp.
  4. Cut each into 4 wedges.
👶 Little ones: cut wedges into strips, and grate the carrot or steam the sticks for 2 min for the younger one. Raw carrot sticks are a choking risk.
🩸 Iron-rich: beans and spinach, with red pepper for vitamin C.
🥡 Lunchbox: 2 tortillas' worth of wedges each, with the veggie sticks. They're good cold or warmed. Matches Thursday's daycare tray (Chicken Quesadillas, Raw Veggies, Fruit, Milk).

Ham & Perogies + Veggies
Thursday kids' dinner → Friday lunch · 2 kid dinners + 2 kid lunches
▾
Allergen-safe (check bag)Kid portions
Allergen-safe: Some perogy dough uses egg, so check the bag. A potato & onion or potato & bacon filling avoids cheese; potato & cheddar is safe once cooked, if the kids will eat it.

Ingredients

  • 600 g frozen perogies, egg-free (about 16)
  • 150 g ham steak, diced iron
  • ½ onion, sliced · 20 g butter
  • 200 g green beans + 60 g carrot, steamed no peas

Method

  1. Boil the perogies as the bag says, until they float, then drain.
  2. Meanwhile, soften the onion in the butter, 5 min. Add the ham and perogies and fry until golden, 4–5 min.
  3. Steam the green beans and carrots.
👶 Little ones: cut perogies into quarters and the ham small for the younger one.
🥡 Lunchbox: 4 perogies with ham and veg each. Pack fruit for vitamin C. Matches Friday's daycare tray (Ham Perogies, Veggies, Fruit, Milk).

Beef Chili
Thursday parents' dinner · from the freezer · 2 parent dinners
▾
ParentsIron-richLow-carb swapAdult spice

Ingredients

  • Your chili from the freezer, 2 portions iron
  • 300 g cauliflower, riced, to serve
  • Optional: ½ cucumber or a few pepper sticks from the week's veg, on the side vit C

Method

  1. Move the chili to the fridge on Wednesday night to thaw.
  2. Reheat in a covered pot with a splash of water, stirring now and then, until bubbling right through, 10–15 min.
  3. Meanwhile, pan-fry the riced cauliflower in a little oil with a pinch of salt, 5 min.
🔄 Low-carb: serve over the riced cauliflower instead of rice or bread.
🌶️ Adult spice: chipotle, cayenne or hot sauce to taste.
🩸 Iron-rich: beef and beans, with fresh veg on the side for vitamin C.

Halloween Cookies to Decorate
Friday afternoon · about 16 cookies, 2–3 each to send
▾
Allergen-safe (check sprinkles)Halloween
Allergen-safe: Most cut-out cookies and royal icing use egg, and decorating kits often carry nut warnings. These cookies are egg-free, with baked butter and milk. Send your own icing and sprinkles too, and ask the daycare to use only those. Check the sprinkles and food colouring for egg, nuts and dairy.

Cookies

  • 90 g soft butter + 70 g sugar
  • 20 ml milk + 1 tsp vanilla
  • 180 g flour + a pinch of salt

Icing (egg-free)

  • 100 g icing sugar + about 15 ml water
  • Orange and black gel food colouring
  • Halloween sprinkles, egg-free & nut-free

Method

  1. Beat the butter and sugar until pale. Beat in the milk and vanilla, then stir in the flour and salt to make a soft dough. Chill 20 min.
  2. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F). Roll out to 5 mm between two sheets of baking paper and cut out pumpkins, ghosts or bats.
  3. Bake 10–12 min, until just golden at the edges. Cool completely and store in a tin.
  4. Stir the icing sugar with the water to a thick, pipeable icing. Split it and tint it. Pack in small lidded tubs with a spoon each, with the sprinkles in a separate tub.
👶 Little ones: a plain cookie with a little icing is plenty for the younger one, and skip hard sprinkles.
🎃 Snack: for Friday's Halloween cookie decorating at daycare. The extras are for decorating at home.
