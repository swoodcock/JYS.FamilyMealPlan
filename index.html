Wieners & Beans + Cheese
Sunday dinner → Monday lunch · serves 4 + lunchbox
▾
Allergen-safeLow-carb swapHidden veg
Allergen-safe: Check the wieners/hot dogs are egg-free (most all-beef ones are). Cheese melted on top is cooked dairy — fine.

Ingredients

  • 8 all-beef wieners, sliced into rounds iron
  • 400 g can baked beans (or kidney/navy beans + a spoon of BBQ sauce)
  • 1 onion, diced · 1 bell pepper, diced hidden veg
  • 100 g shredded cheddar
  • Steamed carrots + green beans no peas
  • Vitamin-C side: orange wedges vit C

Method

  1. Sauté onion and pepper. Add sliced wieners, brown lightly.
  2. Stir in the beans; simmer 10 min.
  3. Top with shredded cheese to melt in. Serve with the steamed veg and orange wedges.
🔄 Low-carb: use a smaller portion of beans, bulked out with extra steamed veg instead.
🩸 Iron: wieners + beans bring some iron — pair with the vitamin-C orange wedges.
🥦 Hidden veg: onion and pepper cooked right into the beans.
🥡 Lunchbox: matches Monday's daycare tray (Weiner & Beans, Shredded Cheese, Veggies, Fruit, Milk).

Chicken & Cauliflower Mac and Cheese
Monday dinner → Tuesday lunch · serves 4 + lunchbox
▾
Allergen-safeHidden vegIron boostLow-carb built in
Allergen-safe: Check the macaroni is egg-free (most dried pasta is). The cheese sauce is cooked milk — fine.

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken breast, diced protein
  • 250 g macaroni, egg-free · 300 g cauliflower florets hidden veg
  • Sauce: 30 g butter + 30 g flour + 500 ml milk + 150 g shredded cheddar
  • 60 g baby spinach, finely chopped iron
  • Salt, pepper, ½ tsp paprika

Method

  1. Boil macaroni and cauliflower florets together until tender. Reserve some cauliflower pieces whole; set aside.
  2. Sear the diced chicken.
  3. Make the cheese sauce: roux, then milk and cheddar. Blend the remaining soft cauliflower straight into the sauce for creaminess.
  4. Stir the sauce, chicken, spinach and reserved cauliflower pieces through the pasta.
🔄 Low-carb built in: this dish is already cauliflower-boosted — for an even lower-carb bowl, swap in extra florets for half the macaroni.
🩸 Iron boost: chicken + spinach — pair with a vitamin-C side.
🥦 Hidden veg: cauliflower blended right into the cheese sauce, spinach wilted in.
🥡 Lunchbox: matches Tuesday's daycare tray (Chicken, Cheese, Cauliflower Macaroni, Fruit, Milk).

Mexican Chicken and Rice
Tuesday dinner → Wednesday lunch · serves 4 + lunchbox
▾
Allergen-safeIron-richHidden vegLow-carb swapAdult spice
Allergen-safe: Naturally egg-free. Cheese topping (optional) is cooked/melted — fine. Check taco seasoning is nut-free.

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken thigh, diced protein
  • 1 onion, diced · 1 bell pepper, diced · 60 g baby spinach, chopped hidden veg
  • 200 g rice (dry)
  • 400 g can diced tomatoes + 200 g corn + 400 g can black beans iron + fiber
  • 1 tbsp mild taco seasoning, 1 tsp cumin
  • Shredded cheese, to top (optional) · Adults: hot sauce / jalapeños

Method

  1. Sear chicken with onion, pepper and spinach.
  2. Add rice, tomatoes, corn, beans, seasoning and enough water/broth to cover; simmer covered ~20 min until the rice is tender.
  3. Top with cheese if using.
🔄 Low-carb: swap the rice for cauliflower rice.
🩸 Iron-rich: chicken + black beans + spinach; tomatoes bring vitamin C.
🌶️ Adults: hot sauce or jalapeños at the table.
🥡 Lunchbox: matches Wednesday's daycare tray (Mexican Chicken and Rice, Fruit, Milk).

Cheeseburger Casserole
Wednesday dinner → Thursday lunch · serves 4 + lunchbox
▾
Allergen-safeIron-richHidden vegLow-carb swap
Allergen-safe: Naturally egg-free (check pasta label to be sure). Cheese topping is cooked/melted — fine.

Ingredients

  • 700 g ground beef iron
  • 1 onion, diced · 2 carrots, grated · 1 zucchini, grated hidden veg
  • 250 g macaroni or egg-free pasta (or cauliflower florets for the low-carb base)
  • 400 g can diced tomatoes + 30 g tomato paste vit C
  • 150 g shredded cheddar
  • Salt, pepper, ½ tsp garlic powder

Method

  1. Brown beef with onion; stir in carrot and zucchini.
  2. Add tomatoes, paste, and cooked pasta (or cauliflower); mix well.
  3. Spread in a baking dish, top with cheese, bake 190°C (375°F) 20 min until bubbly.
🔄 Low-carb: swap the pasta for cauliflower florets.
🩸 Iron-rich: beef + tomato vitamin C to aid absorption.
🥦 Hidden veg: carrot and zucchini folded through.
🥡 Lunchbox: matches Thursday's daycare tray (Cheeseburger Casserole, Veggies, Fruit, Milk).

Pork Loin + Mashed Potatoes
Thursday dinner → Friday lunch · serves 4 + lunchbox
▾
Allergen-safeIron boostLow-carb swap
Allergen-safe: Naturally allergen-free. Mash uses cooked milk and butter — fine.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg pork loin iron
  • Salt, pepper, 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp dried rosemary or thyme, olive oil
  • Mash: 900 g potatoes + butter + milk — plus half a cauliflower head (~300 g) for the low-carb mash
  • Steamed carrots + green beans no peas
  • Vitamin-C side: apple sauce or orange wedges vit C

Method

  1. Season the pork; sear, then roast 180°C (350°F), ~25 min per 500 g, until internal 63°C (145°F). Rest 10 min.
  2. Boil potatoes (and the cauliflower half separately); mash each with butter and milk.
  3. Steam carrots and green beans.
🔄 Low-carb: cauliflower mash instead of potato.
🩸 Iron boost: pork provides some iron — pair with the vitamin-C side.
🥡 Lunchbox: matches Friday's daycare tray (Pork Loin, Mashed Potatoes, Veggies, Fruit, Milk).

Carrot Oatmeal Breakfast Cookies
Monday breakfast · makes ~12
▾
Allergen-safe (egg swap)Hidden veg
Allergen-safe: Standard oatmeal cookies often use egg — this batch uses a flax egg instead (10 g ground flaxseed + 45 ml water, rested 5 min), so it's safe for everyone.

Ingredients

  • 200 g rolled oats
  • 100 g grated carrot hidden veg
  • 80 ml maple syrup or honey
  • 60 ml melted butter or oil
  • 1 flax egg (10 g ground flaxseed + 45 ml water, rested 5 min)
  • 1 tsp cinnamon, ½ tsp baking soda
  • Fresh fruit and water, to serve

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C (350°F). Mix oats, carrot, cinnamon and baking soda.
  2. Stir in the syrup, melted butter and flax egg.
  3. Drop spoonfuls onto a lined tray; bake 12–15 min until set.
  4. Serve with fruit and water.
🥦 Hidden veg: grated carrot baked right in.
🌅 Breakfast: matches Monday's daycare breakfast (Carrot Cake Oatmeal Cookies, Fruit, Water).

Banana Oat Muffins + Cheese
Wednesday breakfast · makes 12
▾
Allergen-safe (egg swap)
Allergen-safe: Standard muffins use egg — this batch uses a flax egg instead (10 g ground flaxseed + 45 ml water, rested 5 min), so it's safe for everyone. Cheese cubes are fine (not raw dairy).

Ingredients

  • 200 g rolled oats, blitzed to a flour (or 200 g flour)
  • 2 ripe bananas, mashed
  • 1 tsp baking powder, ½ tsp cinnamon
  • 60 ml milk
  • 1 flax egg (10 g ground flaxseed + 45 ml water, rested 5 min)
  • 50 g brown sugar
  • Fresh fruit + cheese cubes, to serve · Water

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 190°C (375°F). Mix the oat flour, baking powder and cinnamon.
  2. Whisk the mashed banana, milk, flax egg and sugar; combine with the dry ingredients.
  3. Divide into a muffin tin; bake 18–20 min until a tester comes out clean.
  4. Serve with fruit, cheese cubes and water.
🌅 Breakfast: matches Wednesday's daycare breakfast (Muffins, Fruit, Cheese, Water).

Scrambled Eggs (or Tofu) + Bagels
Thursday breakfast · serves 4
▾
Allergen-safe (alt. needed)Iron boost
Allergen-safe: Scrambled eggs are fundamentally egg-based and can't be reasonably adapted — for the egg-free portion, a turmeric tofu scramble mimics the look and texture closely.

Ingredients

  • 6 eggs + 60 g shredded cheese, for the family's scrambled eggs
  • 300 g firm tofu, crumbled + ¼ tsp turmeric + pinch salt, for the egg-free scramble iron
  • Bagels, egg-free
  • Juice

Method

  1. For the family: whisk eggs, scramble in a pan, fold in cheese.
  2. For the egg-free portion: sauté the crumbled tofu with turmeric and salt 5 min until warmed through and golden.
  3. Toast bagels; serve both with juice.
🩸 Iron boost: tofu brings a bit of iron to the egg-free portion.
🌅 Breakfast: matches Thursday's daycare breakfast for the rest of the family; the allergy-safe portion gets tofu scramble instead of egg.

Cinnamon Buns
Thursday snack · makes 12
▾
Allergen-safe (egg swap)
Allergen-safe: Yeasted dough often uses egg — this batch uses a flax egg instead (10 g ground flaxseed + 45 ml water, rested 5 min), so it's safe for everyone.

Ingredients

  • 500 g bread flour, 7 g instant yeast, 50 g sugar, ½ tsp salt
  • 250 ml warm milk, 50 g melted butter
  • 1 flax egg (10 g ground flaxseed + 45 ml water, rested 5 min)
  • Filling: 80 g brown sugar, 2 tsp cinnamon, 40 g softened butter
  • Icing (optional): 100 g powdered sugar + 20 ml milk
  • Fresh fruit and milk, to serve

Method

  1. Mix flour, yeast, sugar and salt. Add warm milk, melted butter and the flax egg; knead into a soft dough. Rise 1 hr.
  2. Roll out, spread with softened butter and cinnamon sugar. Roll up and slice into 12 buns.
  3. Rise 30 min more; bake 180°C (350°F) 20–25 min until golden. Drizzle with icing if using. Best made ahead — bake Wednesday evening for Thursday's snack.
  4. Serve with fruit and milk.
🍪 Snack: matches Thursday's daycare snack (Cinnamon Buns, Fruit, Milk).

Egg-Free French Toast
Friday breakfast · serves 4
▾
Allergen-safe
Allergen-safe: Classic French toast dips bread in egg — this batter uses cornstarch + milk instead, so no egg is needed for anyone.

Ingredients

  • 8 slices bread, egg-free
  • 250 ml milk, 30 g cornstarch, 1 tsp vanilla extract, 1 tsp cinnamon
  • Butter, for frying
  • Fresh fruit and milk, to serve

Method

  1. Whisk milk, cornstarch, vanilla and cinnamon into a smooth batter.
  2. Dip bread slices, coating both sides.
  3. Fry in butter until golden on each side.
  4. Serve with fruit and milk.
🌅 Breakfast: matches Friday's daycare breakfast (French Toast, Fruit, Milk) — egg-free for everyone, no separate portion needed.

Dairy-Free Frozen Yogurt Pops
Friday snack · makes ~8 pops
▾
Allergen-safe
Allergen-safe: Standard yogurt pops are raw/uncooked dairy — this version uses dairy-free coconut yogurt, so it's safe for everyone with no separate portion needed.

Ingredients

  • 500 g dairy-free coconut or oat yogurt
  • 200 g mixed berries or fruit purée

Method
