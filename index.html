Mexican Lasagna
Monday dinner → Tuesday lunch · 2 kid dinners + 2 kid lunches
▾
Allergen-safeIron-richHidden vegKid portions
Allergen-safe: Layered with corn tortillas instead of lasagna noodles, so there's no egg pasta to worry about. No sour cream on top; the cheese is all melted in.

Ingredients

  • 230 g ground beef iron
  • ½ onion, diced · ½ bell pepper, diced vit C
  • 20 g baby spinach, finely chopped hidden veg
  • ½ can (400 g) black beans, drained & rinsed iron + fiber — the other half goes in Wednesday's stew
  • ½ can (400 g) diced tomatoes vit C — the other half goes in Wednesday's stew
  • 1 tsp mild taco seasoning
  • 5 small corn tortillas, egg-free
  • 80 g shredded cheddar

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 190°C (375°F). Brown the beef with the onion and pepper, then stir in the spinach until it wilts.
  2. Add the taco seasoning, tomatoes and beans; simmer 8–10 min until thick.
  3. In a small loaf pan or 20 cm dish, layer tortillas (tear to fit), meat and cheese, three times, finishing with cheese.
  4. Bake 20 min until bubbling. Rest 5 min before cutting so the squares hold together.
🩸 Iron-rich: beef, black beans and spinach, with tomato and pepper bringing vitamin C.
🥡 Lunchbox: a firm square reheats cleanly. Matches Tuesday's daycare tray (Mexican Lasagna, Fruit, Milk).
2
Chicken Alfredo Pasta
Tuesday dinner → Wednesday lunch · 2 kid dinners + 2 kid lunches
▾
Allergen-safe (pasta swap)Iron-richHidden vegKid portions
Allergen-safe: Tortellini is fresh egg pasta, so this uses a dried egg-free short pasta instead. Last week's macaroni works, or penne. The Alfredo sauce is simmered cream and melted parmesan, so it's cooked dairy.

Ingredients

  • 170 g chicken breast, diced small
  • 90 g egg-free short pasta (macaroni or penne)
  • Sauce: 15 g butter + 1 small clove garlic + 100 ml heavy cream + 30 g grated parmesan
  • 100 g broccoli, in small florets vit C
  • 20 g baby spinach, finely chopped iron
  • Lunchbox veggies: 2 celery stalks, as sticks

Method

  1. Cook the pasta, adding the broccoli for the last 3 min; drain.
  2. Pan-cook the chicken in a little oil until cooked through.
  3. Sauce: melt the butter, soften the garlic 30 s, add the cream and simmer 3 min, then whisk in the parmesan until smooth.
  4. Toss everything with the sauce and stir in the spinach until it wilts. Add a splash of pasta water if it's thick.
🩸 Iron-rich: chicken and spinach, with broccoli for vitamin C.
🥡 Lunchbox: add a splash of milk before reheating to loosen the sauce. Pack with the celery sticks. Matches Wednesday's daycare tray (Tortellini with Chicken & Alfredo Sauce, Veggies, Fruit, Milk).
3
Bean & Sausage Stew + Buns
Wednesday dinner → Thursday lunch · 2 kid dinners + 2 kid lunches
▾
Allergen-safe (check labels)Iron-richHidden vegKid portions
Allergen-safe: No egg or dairy in the stew. Check the sausage and buns: some sausages use egg as a binder, and some soft buns are glazed with egg.

Ingredients

  • 150 g smoked sausage, egg-free, sliced into half-moons iron
  • ½ onion, diced · 1 small carrot (~60 g) and 1 celery stalk, finely diced hidden veg
  • ½ can black beans + ½ can chickpeas, drained & rinsed iron + fiber — the halves left from Monday and Tuesday
  • ½ can diced tomatoes vit C — the half left from Monday
  • 300 ml chicken broth · pinch of thyme · 1 bay leaf
  • 4 small dinner buns, egg-free

Method

  1. Soften the onion, carrot and celery in a little oil, 6–8 min. Add the sausage and brown lightly.
  2. Add the beans, chickpeas, tomatoes, broth, thyme and bay leaf. Simmer 20–25 min until thickened.
  3. Take out the bay leaf. Mash a few spoonfuls of beans against the side of the pot to thicken it for little spoons.
🩸 Iron-rich: sausage, black beans and chickpeas, with tomato for vitamin C.
🥡 Lunchbox: stew in a thermos, bun wrapped separately. Matches Thursday's daycare tray (Bean Stew, Buns, Fruit, Milk).
4
Meatloaf + Mashed Potatoes
Thursday dinner → Friday lunch · 2 kid dinners + 2 kid lunches
▾
Allergen-safe (egg swap)Iron-richHidden vegKid portions
Allergen-safe: Meatloaf is normally bound with egg. This uses a half flax egg instead (5 g ground flaxseed + 20 ml water, rested 5 min). The milk soaks into the breadcrumbs and bakes in, and the mash uses cooked milk and butter.

Meatloaf

  • 300 g ground beef iron
  • Binder: 30 g breadcrumbs, egg-free + 30 ml milk + ½ flax egg
  • ¼ onion and 40 g carrot, finely grated · 30 g baby spinach, finely chopped hidden veg
  • Salt and pepper
  • Extra glaze: 50 g ketchup + 10 g tomato paste + a splash of Worcestershire vit C

Mash and side

  • 400 g potatoes + 15 g butter + 50 ml milk
  • 100 g green beans no peas

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 190°C (375°F). Soak the breadcrumbs in the milk for a few minutes and mix up the flax egg.
  2. Mix the beef, soaked crumbs, flax egg, grated veg, spinach, salt and pepper. Shape into a small loaf on a lined tray.
  3. Stir the glaze together. Brush half over the loaf and bake 35–40 min, until the centre reaches 71°C (160°F). Brush on the rest for the last 10 min. Rest 5 min before slicing.
  4. Meanwhile, boil the potatoes until tender and mash with the butter and milk. Steam the green beans.
📝 From the Recipe Book: "A real family favorite — make extra glaze next time," so the glaze is doubled for this size of loaf.
🩸 Iron-rich: beef and spinach, with the tomato glaze for vitamin C.
🥡 Lunchbox: a slice with a scoop of mash and the green beans. Matches Friday's daycare tray (Meatloaf, Mashed Potatoes, Veggies, Fruit, Milk).

Chickpea "Egg Salad" Sandwiches
Tuesday snack · 2 sandwiches, cut into quarters
▾
Allergen-safe (egg swap)Iron boost
Allergen-safe: Egg salad can't be made without egg, so this is mashed chickpea with a pinch of turmeric for the yellow colour. Regular mayo is made with egg, so use an egg-free (vegan) mayo.

Ingredients

  • ½ can chickpeas, drained & rinsed iron — the other half goes in Wednesday's stew
  • 2 tbsp egg-free mayo · ½ tsp mild mustard · ¼ tsp turmeric · pinch of salt
  • ½ celery stalk, very finely diced
  • 4 slices sandwich bread, egg-free
  • Veggies: ½ cucumber, as sticks
  • Milk, to drink

Method

  1. Mash the chickpeas with a fork until mostly smooth, with a few small pieces left.
  2. Stir in the mayo, mustard, turmeric, salt and celery. The filling keeps 3 days in the fridge, so it can be made Monday.
  3. Spread on the bread, cut off the crusts if you like, and cut into quarters. Pack with the cucumber sticks.
🍪 Snack: matches Tuesday's daycare snack (Egg Salad Sandwiches, Veggies, Milk).

Seed Trail Mix + Raw Veggies
Wednesday snack · 2 portions, plus a jar for later
▾
Allergen-safe (nut swap)Iron boost
Allergen-safe: Trail mix is normally built on peanuts and tree nuts. This uses sunflower and pumpkin seeds instead. Check the bags for a "may contain peanuts or tree nuts" warning.

Ingredients

  • 60 g roasted sunflower seeds, unsalted
  • 60 g roasted pumpkin seeds iron
  • 40 g raisins
  • Raw veggies: 2 celery stalks + ½ cucumber, as sticks
  • Milk, to drink

Method

  1. Mix the seeds and raisins. Pack about 30 g per child and keep the rest in a jar.
  2. Pack with the veggie sticks.
👶 Little ones: whole seeds and raisins can be a choking risk for very young eaters. For the younger one, chop the raisins and give the seeds roughly ground.
🍪 Snack: matches Wednesday's daycare snack (Trail Mix, Raw Veggies, Milk).
🍪
Sugar-Free Pudding + Animal Crackers
Thursday snack · 2 cups
▾
Allergen-safe
Allergen-safe: This is a cornstarch pudding, not an egg custard, and the milk is fully cooked. Check the animal crackers are egg-free and nut-free.

Ingredients

  • 250 ml milk
  • 15 g cornstarch
  • Sugar substitute (stevia or erythritol), to taste · ½ tsp vanilla
  • Animal crackers, egg-free & nut-free
  • Fresh fruit and water, to serve

Method

  1. Whisk the milk, cornstarch and sweetener in a small pan until smooth.
  2. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until it thickens and just bubbles, 4–5 min.
  3. Off the heat, stir in the vanilla. Pour into two lidded cups, press cling film on the surface, and chill at least 2 hrs.
  4. Pack the crackers separately so they stay crisp.
🍪 Snack: matches Thursday's daycare snack (Sugar Free Pudding, Animal Crackers, Fruit, Water). Make Wednesday evening.
🍪
Pizza Bombs
Friday snack · makes 8, freeze the extras
▾
Allergen-safe (check labels)
Allergen-safe: Pizza dough is usually egg-free, but check the label. The usual egg wash is replaced by a brush of milk. The mozzarella is baked inside.

Ingredients

  • 1 ball pizza dough (~450 g), egg-free — use half, and freeze the rest
  • 60 g shredded mozzarella
  • 30 g pepperoni, chopped small
  • 3 tbsp pizza sauce or marinara, plus a little for dipping
  • 1 tbsp milk, for brushing — not egg
  • Water, to drink

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 190°C (375°F); it can share the oven with the meatloaf. Cut the half-ball of dough into 8 pieces and flatten each into a small round.
  2. Top each with a little sauce, a pinch of mozzarella and some pepperoni. Pinch the dough closed and set seam-side down on a lined tray.
  3. Brush with milk and bake 15–18 min until golden. Cool, then pack two each and freeze the rest.
🍪 Snack: matches Friday's daycare snack (Pizza Bombs, Water). Bake Thursday evening.
