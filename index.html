Chili with Cheese
Sunday dinner → Monday lunch · 2 kid dinners + 2 kid lunches
▾
Allergen-safeIron-richHidden vegKid portions
Allergen-safe: Naturally egg-free. The cheese goes on hot to melt, so it's cooked dairy — fine.
✅ Already made: this one's done and in the fridge, so nothing for it appears on the shopping list. Reheat and portion — two servings for Sunday dinner, two packed for Monday.

Ingredients

  • 200 g ground beef iron
  • ½ onion, diced
  • 400 g can kidney or black beans, drained & rinsed iron + fiber
  • 400 g can diced tomatoes vit C
  • 1 tsp mild chili powder, ½ tsp ground cumin
  • 50 g shredded cheddar, to melt on top

Method

  1. Brown the beef with the onion; drain any excess fat.
  2. Stir in the chili powder and cumin, then the tomatoes and beans.
  3. Simmer 20 min until thickened. Top each bowl with cheddar and let it melt in.
🩸 Iron-rich: beef plus beans, with the tomatoes bringing the vitamin C to help it absorb.
🥡 Lunchbox: matches Monday's daycare tray (Chili with Cheese, Fruit, Milk).

Chicken Broccoli Rice Casserole
Monday dinner → Tuesday lunch · 2 kid dinners + 2 kid lunches
▾
Allergen-safeHidden vegKid portions
Allergen-safe: Built on a butter-and-flour cheese sauce rather than canned condensed soup, so there's no egg anywhere and the milk is cooked.
👶 Sized for kids: makes 4 kid-sized portions — two for dinner, two packed for Tuesday.
🔪 From the weekend batch: the 200 g of chicken here was poached with the soup on the weekend and kept in the fridge.

Ingredients

  • 200 g cooked chicken breast, shredded weekend batch
  • 150 g broccoli florets, cut small hidden veg
  • 100 g rice (dry), cooked
  • Sauce: 15 g butter + 15 g flour + 200 ml milk + 60 g shredded cheddar
  • Salt, pepper, pinch of garlic powder

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 190°C (375°F). Cook the rice; steam the broccoli until just tender.
  2. Make the sauce: melt the butter, whisk in the flour, add the milk and simmer until thickened, then stir in the cheddar.
  3. Fold the rice, broccoli and chicken through the sauce; season.
  4. Spread in a small baking dish and bake 15–20 min until bubbling.
🥡 Lunchbox: matches Tuesday's daycare tray (Chicken Broccoli Rice Casserole, Fruit, Milk).

Lasagna
Tuesday dinner → Wednesday lunch · whole family, serves 4 + lunchbox
▾
Allergen-safe (check noodles)Iron-richHidden vegLow-carb swapAdult spiceWhole family
Allergen-safe: Check the lasagna noodles. Plain dried sheets are usually just semolina and water, but fresh and "egg pasta" sheets contain egg. The ricotta and mozzarella are baked, so they count as cooked dairy.

Ingredients

  • 500 g ground beef iron
  • 1 onion, diced · 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 700 g passata or crushed tomatoes + 30 g tomato paste vit C
  • 1 tsp dried Italian herbs, salt, pepper
  • 100 g spinach, chopped iron + hidden veg
  • 250 g ricotta
  • 200 g mozzarella, shredded · 60 g parmesan, grated
  • 250 g lasagna noodles, egg-free — or 2 zucchini, sliced lengthways, for the low-carb version

Method

  1. Brown the beef with the onion and garlic. Add the tomatoes, paste and herbs; simmer 20 min, then stir the spinach through to wilt.
  2. Layer in a baking dish: sauce, noodles, ricotta, mozzarella — repeat, finishing with mozzarella and the parmesan.
  3. Bake 190°C (375°F) 35–40 min until golden and bubbling. Rest 10 min before cutting, or it won't hold its shape.
🔄 Low-carb: replace the pasta sheets with lengthways-sliced zucchini. Salt the slices and pat them dry first so the bake doesn't go watery.
🩸 Iron-rich: beef and spinach together, with tomato for the vitamin C.
🌶️ Adults: chili flakes at the table — pull the kids' portions first.
🥡 Lunchbox: matches Wednesday's daycare tray (Lasagna, Fruit, Milk).

Chicken Quesadillas
Wednesday dinner → Thursday lunch · 2 kid dinners + 2 kid lunches
▾
Allergen-safeRaw veg sideKid portions
Allergen-safe: Flour tortillas are normally egg-free — check the label. The cheese is melted, so it's cooked dairy.
👶 Sized for kids: makes 4 kid-sized portions — two for dinner, two packed for Thursday.
🔪 From the weekend batch: this chicken was poached with the soup and frozen — move it to the fridge Tuesday night so it's ready.

Ingredients

  • 250 g cooked chicken, shredded weekend batch, thawed
  • 8 small flour tortillas, egg-free
  • 120 g shredded cheddar
  • ½ tsp mild taco seasoning
  • Butter or oil, for the pan
  • To serve: raw carrot and cucumber sticks, sliced avocado, a squeeze of lime, chopped cilantro

Method

  1. Toss the shredded chicken with the taco seasoning.
  2. Scatter cheese and chicken over half of each tortilla, fold, and cook in a lightly buttered pan over medium heat until golden and the cheese has melted, about 2 min a side.
  3. Cut into wedges. Serve with the raw veggie sticks and avocado.
🥡 Lunchbox: matches Thursday's daycare tray (Chicken Quesadillas, Raw Veggies, Fruit, Milk). They pack well cut into wedges.

Ham Perogies
Thursday dinner → Friday lunch · whole family, serves 4 + lunchbox
▾
Allergen-safe (check dough)Hidden vegLow-carb swapWhole family
Allergen-safe: Check the perogie dough — most frozen ones are flour and water, but some brands add egg. The potato-and-cheese filling is cooked, so that part is fine.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg frozen potato & cheese perogies, egg-free
  • 250 g ham, diced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 40 g butter, for the pan
  • 200 g green beans no peas + 2 carrots, sliced hidden veg
  • Adults: sour cream, to serve raw dairy

Method

  1. Boil the perogies until they float, about 4 min; drain well.
  2. Melt the butter in a large pan and soften the onion. Add the ham and brown lightly.
  3. Add the drained perogies and pan-fry until golden on both sides.
  4. Steam the green beans and carrots alongside.
🔄 Low-carb: a smaller perogie portion bulked out with extra steamed veg, or pan-fried cabbage in place of half the perogies.
⚠️ Sour cream: uncooked dairy — keep it to the adults' plates only.
🥡 Lunchbox: matches Friday's daycare tray (Ham Perogies, Veggies, Fruit, Milk).

Big Mac Salad
Sunday · parents only · makes 3 servings
▾
Parents onlyNaturally low-carb
🥡 Make-ahead: browns and portions well. Keep the sauce in its own jar and dress only the bowl you're about to eat — dressed lettuce is soggy by the next day. Good for 3–4 days.

Ingredients — salad

  • 450 g ground beef
  • 1 tsp sea salt, ½ tsp ground black pepper
  • 1 small head iceberg lettuce, finely chopped
  • 85 g cheddar, shredded
  • ¼ white onion, finely diced
  • 75 g pickle slices

Ingredients — burger sauce

  • 120 g Greek yogurt
  • 60 g ketchup · 15 g Dijon mustard · 30 g dill relish
  • 1 clove garlic, grated
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika · ¼ tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp Frank's RedHot, to taste

Method

  1. Cook the beef in a large skillet over medium heat with the salt and pepper, crumbling as it goes, until browned through.
  2. Whisk all the sauce ingredients together in a separate bowl. Keep it in its own jar rather than dressing the salad.
  3. Divide the lettuce, beef, cheese, onion and pickles between bowls and spoon the sauce over at the table. For meal-prep, pack the three portions with the sauce separate.
🔄 Low-GI: already low-carb. The only real sugar is in the ketchup and relish — no-sugar-added versions cut most of it.
Source: adapted from Fed & Fit, converted to metric.

Chicken Spinach Artichoke Soup
Monday · parents only · makes 6 servings
▾
Parents onlyNaturally low-carb
🔪 Poach the week's chicken here: simmer the full 1 kg in the broth, then lift out 450 g before the cheese goes in — 200 g to the fridge for Monday's casserole, 250 g to the freezer for Wednesday's quesadillas. The mozzarella, parmesan and spinach are the same ones Tuesday's lasagna needs.

Ingredients

  • 15 ml extra-virgin olive oil
  • ½ yellow onion, chopped · 2 celery stalks, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced · ½ tsp red pepper flakes
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper
  • 1.4 L chicken broth
  • Juice of ½ lemon
  • 570 g boneless skinless chicken breasts (about 3 large) — use 1 kg to poach for the week, see above
  • 400 g can artichoke hearts, drained and quartered
  • 110 g mozzarella, shredded · 90 g parmesan, grated, plus more to finish
  • 30 ml heavy cream (optional)
  • 120 g fresh spinach
  • 2 tbsp parsley, chopped, to garnish

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium. Soften the onion and celery, about 6 min. Add the garlic and pepper flakes and cook 1 min more.
  2. Pour in the broth and lemon juice and bring to a boil.
  3. Add the chicken breasts, lower the heat, and simmer until cooked through, 10–12 min.
  4. Lift the chicken out and shred it with two forks. Set aside 450 g for the kids' dishes now — fridge 200 g, freeze 250 g — then return the rest to the pot.
  5. Add the artichokes, mozzarella, parmesan and cream if using. Cook, stirring, until the cheese melts.
  6. Stir in the spinach and serve with parsley and extra parmesan.
🔄 Low-GI: no starch in the pot — it's already a low-carb dinner as written.
🥡 Make-ahead: keeps best of the three — 4–5 days in the fridge, or freeze portions. Reheat gently so the cheese doesn't split.
Source: adapted from Delish, converted to metric.

Hot Honey Shrimp Cobb Salad
Wednesday · parents only · makes 3 servings
▾
Parents onlyNaturally low-carb
⚠️ Contains egg: the hard-boiled eggs make this one to keep well away from the kids' quesadillas — separate board, separate plate, and wash up in between.
🥡 Make-ahead: cook the shrimp, blend the dressing and pack the components now, but cut the avocado fresh on the day and keep the dressing separate. Eat this one first of the three — 2 days at best.

Ingredients — shrimp

  • 450 g shrimp, frozen or fresh
  • 10 ml olive oil
  • 2 tsp chili powder · 1 tsp salt · ½ tsp dried oregano · ½ tsp garlic powder
  • 30 g hot honey · 30 ml fresh lime juice

Ingredients — dressing

  • 240 g Greek yogurt
  • ½ cup fresh cilantro (about 15 g)
  • 1 jalapeño, stem removed
  • 60 ml lime juice (about 2 limes) · ½ tsp sea salt

Ingredients — salad

  • 1 head romaine, chopped
  • 150 g cherry tomatoes
  • 1 large avocado, cut into 1 cm pieces
  • 75 g cheddar, shredded
  • 3 rashers cooked bacon, chopped
  • 3 hard-boiled eggs, halved
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges · salt and pepper

Method

  1. Put the shrimp in a large skillet with the olive oil over medium-high heat. Stir continuously until they thaw and turn opaque.
  2. Add the chili powder, salt, oregano, garlic powder, hot honey and lime juice. Toss and keep cooking until the shrimp curl tightly and are opaque through, 10–15 min in total.
  3. Blend the yogurt, cilantro, jalapeño, lime juice and salt until smooth.
  4. Build the bowls with the lettuce, tomatoes, avocado, cheese, bacon, egg halves and shrimp. Season, add lime wedges, and dress at the table.
🔄 Low-GI: the hot honey is the only sugar — use it sparingly on the diabetic portion, or dress those shrimp with lime and chili alone.
Source: adapted from Fed & Fit, converted to metric.

Frozen Yogurt Sandwiches
Monday snack · makes ~8
▾
Allergen-safe (dairy + egg swaps)
Allergen-safe: Two swaps in one. Frozen yogurt is still uncooked dairy even frozen, so this uses dairy-free coconut or oat yogurt; and the cookies use a flax egg. That makes the whole batch safe for everyone, with no separate version.
🔪 Shared prep: bake one batch of the oatmeal raisin cookies on Sunday — half go in here, half are Wednesday's snack.

Ingredients

  • 400 g dairy-free coconut or oat yogurt
  • 100 g berries or fruit purée
  • Honey or maple syrup, to taste
  • 16 egg-free oatmeal cookies (see Wednesday's snack)
  • Fresh fruit and water, to serve

Method

  1. Blend the yogurt with the fruit and a little honey.
  2. Spread a thick layer onto the flat side of a cookie and sandwich a second on top.
  3. Wrap each one and freeze at least 4 hrs — best made Sunday so they're solid by Monday.
  4. Pack frozen, with fruit and water.
🍪 Snack: matches Monday's daycare snack (Frozen yogurt sandwiches, Fruit, Water).

Oatmeal Raisin Cookies
Wednesday snack · makes ~24
▾
Allergen-safe (egg swap)
Allergen-safe: Standard cookies use egg — this batch uses a flax egg instead (10 g ground flaxseed + 45 ml water, rested 5 min), so it's safe for everyone.
🔪 Bake once, use twice: this batch covers both Monday's frozen yogurt sandwiches and Wednesday's snack. Make it Sunday.

Ingredients

  • 200 g flour · 150 g rolled oats
  • 120 g butter, softened
  • 100 g brown sugar · 50 g granulated sugar
  • 1 flax egg (10 g ground flaxseed + 45 ml water, rested 5 min)
  • 1 tsp baking soda · 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 80 g raisins
  • Fresh fruit and milk, to serve

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C (350°F). Cream the butter and sugars, then beat in the flax egg.
  2. Fold in the flour, oats, baking soda and cinnamon, then the raisins.
  3. Scoop onto lined trays and bake 11–13 min until set at the edges. Cool fully before storing or sandwiching.
  4. Serve with fruit and milk.
🍪 Snack: matches Wednesday's daycare snack (Cookies, Fruit, Milk).
