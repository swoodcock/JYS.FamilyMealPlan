Chicken Pot Pie
Monday dinner → Tuesday lunch · serves 4 + lunchbox
▾
Allergen-safe (2 swaps)Iron boostHidden vegLow-carb swapAdult spice
Allergen-safe — two swaps: classic pot pie is built on peas and carrots, so the peas are replaced with green beans and corn. And the lid is normally brushed with beaten egg — this uses milk instead, which browns nearly as well. Also check the pastry itself is egg-free; most puff and shortcrust sheets are, but not all.

Ingredients

  • 700 g chicken thighs or breast, diced iron
  • 1 onion, diced · 2 carrots, diced · 2 celery stalks, diced hidden veg
  • 150 g green beans, cut short + 150 g frozen corn no peas
  • 50 g butter · 50 g flour
  • 500 ml chicken broth · 250 ml milk
  • 1 tsp dried thyme, salt, pepper
  • 1 sheet puff pastry or shortcrust, egg-free
  • 2 tbsp milk, for brushing — not egg
  • Vitamin-C side: orange wedges vit C
  • For Tuesday's lunchbox: 2 buns, egg-free

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F).
  2. Melt the butter in a large pan and soften the onion, carrot and celery, 6–8 min.
  3. Add the chicken and cook until it's no longer pink on the outside.
  4. Sprinkle in the flour and stir for a minute. Gradually add the broth, then the milk, stirring as it thickens; simmer 5 min.
  5. Stir in the green beans, corn and thyme; season. If making the low-carb portion, spoon it out now into a small dish.
  6. Pour the rest into a baking dish. Lay the pastry over, trim, press the edges down and cut a few steam slits. Brush with milk.
  7. Bake 25–30 min until the pastry is puffed and golden.
🔄 Low-carb: the pastry is where the carbs are. Serve that portion as filling only, spooned out before the lid goes on, with extra green beans alongside.
🩸 Iron boost: chicken — pair with the vitamin-C orange wedges.
🥦 Hidden veg: carrot, celery, green beans and corn all cooked into the filling.
🌶️ Adults: cracked black pepper or hot sauce at the table.
🥡 Lunchbox: matches Tuesday's daycare tray (Chicken Pot Pie, Buns, Fruit, Milk) — pack with a bun and some fruit.
2
Ham Potato Soup
Tuesday dinner → Wednesday lunch · serves 4 + lunchbox
▾
Allergen-safeIron boostHidden vegLow-carb swapAdult spice
Allergen-safe: Nothing to swap. The milk is heated through and the cheese is melted in, so both are cooked dairy, and there's no egg or peas anywhere.

Ingredients

  • 30 g butter
  • 1 onion, diced · 2 carrots, diced · 2 celery stalks, diced hidden veg
  • 30 g flour
  • 1 L chicken broth
  • 800 g potatoes, peeled & diced — or 400 g potatoes + 400 g cauliflower for the low-carb version
  • 400 g cooked ham, diced iron
  • 250 ml milk
  • 100 g shredded cheddar (optional)
  • 1 bay leaf, ½ tsp dried thyme, salt, pepper
  • Vitamin-C side: orange wedges vit C

Method

  1. Melt the butter in a large pot and soften the onion, carrot and celery, 6–8 min.
  2. Stir in the flour for a minute, then gradually whisk in the broth.
  3. Add the potatoes, bay leaf and thyme. Simmer 15–20 min until the potatoes are tender.
  4. Mash a few potato pieces against the side of the pot to thicken the soup.
  5. Stir in the ham and milk and heat through without letting it boil. Add the cheddar if using, and stir until melted.
  6. Remove the bay leaf and season.
🔄 Low-carb: swap half the potatoes for cauliflower florets. They cook down and disappear into the soup, so nobody notices.
🩸 Iron boost: ham brings some iron — pair with the orange wedges.
🌶️ Adults: hot sauce and black pepper at the table.
🥡 Lunchbox: matches Wednesday's daycare tray (Ham Potato Soup, Bologna Sandwich, Fruit, Milk). Pack the soup in a thermos with a ham or turkey sandwich in place of the bologna:
  • 4 slices sandwich bread, egg-free
  • ~100 g sliced ham or turkey
  • A little butter

Dairy-Free Yogurt + Graham Wafers
Monday snack · serves 4
▾
Allergen-safe (dairy swap)
Allergen-safe: Regular yogurt is uncooked dairy, so this uses dairy-free coconut or oat yogurt for everyone. Check the graham wafers are egg-free.

Ingredients

  • 400 g dairy-free coconut or oat yogurt
  • Graham wafers, egg-free
  • Fresh fruit — berries or sliced banana, to top
  • Milk, to drink

Method

  1. Spoon the yogurt into small lidded containers and top with fruit.
  2. Pack the graham wafers separately so they stay crisp.
  3. Send with milk.
🍪 Snack: matches Monday's daycare snack (Yogurt, Graham Wafers, Fruit, Milk).

Allergen-safe
Allergen-safe: The muffins are your own frozen batch, so no baking this week. Ham needs no swap, and firm cheese served cold is treated the same as the cheese cubes in earlier weeks.

Ingredients

  • ~100 g sliced deli ham
  • ~100 g firm cheese, cubed

Seed Trail Mix + Raw Veggies
Wednesday snack · serves 4
▾
Allergen-safe
Allergen-safe: Standard trail mix is built on tree nuts and peanuts — this seed-based version is safe for everyone, so there's no separate batch.

Ingredients

  • 100 g roasted sunflower seeds
  • 100 g roasted pumpkin seeds
  • 50 g dried cranberries or raisins
  • Raw veggie sticks: celery, carrot, cucumber
  • Milk, to drink

Method

  1. Mix the seeds and dried fruit; portion into snack bags. Keeps all week at room temperature.
  2. Pack with the veggie sticks and milk.
