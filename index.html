Meatballs + Rice + Veggies
Sunday dinner → Monday lunch · 2 kid dinners + 2 kid lunches
▾
Allergen-safe (no egg)Iron-rich ★Hidden vegKid portions
Allergen-safe: Breadcrumbs soaked in milk bind the meatballs instead of egg, and the milk is baked in. Check the breadcrumbs are egg-free. No parmesan.

Ingredients

  • 350 g ground beef iron ★
  • 25 g breadcrumbs, egg-free, soaked in 30 ml milk
  • ¼ onion + 1 clove garlic, grated · 30 g carrot, finely grated hidden veg
  • ½ tsp dried oregano, a pinch of salt
  • Sauce: 1 small can (398 ml) crushed tomatoes vit C
  • 120 g rice (dry)
  • 150 g broccoli vit C + 50 g carrot, steamed

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 200°C (400°F). Start the rice.
  2. Mix the beef, soaked crumbs, onion, garlic, carrot, oregano and salt with your hands. Roll into 2–3 cm balls (about 20).
  3. Bake on a lined tray 15–18 min, until cooked through.
  4. Warm the crushed tomatoes in a pan, add the meatballs and simmer 5 min. Steam the broccoli and carrots.
👶 Little ones: quarter the meatballs for the younger one; round, firm foods are a choking risk.
🩸 Iron-rich ★: beef, with tomato and broccoli for vitamin C.
🥡 Lunchbox: meatballs and sauce over rice, veg on the side. Matches Monday's daycare tray (Meatballs, Rice, Veggies, Fruit, Milk).

Cheeseburger Casserole
Make Sunday Nov 1 and freeze → Thursday Nov 12 lunch · 4 kid portions
▾
Allergen-safe (check pasta)Iron-richHidden vegKid portions
Allergen-safe: No egg or raw dairy. Check the macaroni isn't egg pasta. It's made without cheese, since the kids don't like it. If you want some, stir 40 g of shredded cheddar into one tub before freezing; it's fine once melted.

Ingredients

  • 300 g ground beef iron
  • ½ onion, finely chopped · 60 g carrot, finely grated hidden veg
  • 1 small can (398 ml) crushed tomatoes + 20 g tomato paste vit C
  • 1 tbsp ketchup + 1 tsp mild mustard
  • 150 g macaroni, egg-free

Method

  1. Cook the macaroni 2 min less than the packet says (it softens when reheated). Drain.
  2. Brown the beef with the onion and carrot, 6–7 min. Stir in the tomato paste, tomatoes, ketchup and mustard and simmer 10 min.
  3. Stir in the macaroni. Cool, then pack into 2 freezer tubs of 2 portions each and label with the date.
  4. Nov 11 evening: move one tub to the fridge. Nov 12 morning: reheat with a splash of water until piping hot and pack in a warmed thermos.
🩸 Iron-rich: beef, with tomato for vitamin C.
🥡 Lunchbox: with veggie sticks on the side. Matches Nov 12's daycare tray (Cheeseburger Casserole, Veggies, Fruit, Milk). The second tub can be Friday Nov 13's freezer lunch.
2
Chicken Pot Pie + Buns
Monday dinner → Tuesday lunch · 2 kid dinners + 2 kid lunches
▾
Allergen-safe (2 swaps)Iron boostHidden vegKid portions
Allergen-safe — two swaps: classic pot pie is built on peas and carrots, so the peas are replaced with green beans and corn. And the lid is normally brushed with beaten egg; this uses milk instead, which browns nearly as well. Also check the pastry itself is egg-free; most puff and shortcrust sheets are, but not all. Check the buns are egg-free too.

Ingredients

  • 350 g boneless chicken thighs, diced iron
  • ½ onion, diced · 100 g carrot, diced · 2 celery stalks, diced hidden veg
  • 80 g green beans, cut short + 80 g frozen corn no peas
  • 25 g butter · 25 g flour
  • 250 ml chicken broth · 150 ml milk
  • ½ tsp dried thyme, salt, pepper
  • 1 sheet puff pastry or shortcrust, egg-free, thawed
  • 1 tbsp milk, for brushing — not egg
  • For Tuesday's lunchbox: 2–4 buns, egg-free

Method

  1. Thaw the pastry in the fridge during the day (or 30–40 min on the counter). Preheat the oven to 200°C (400°F).
  2. Melt the butter in a pan and soften the onion, carrot and celery, 6–8 min.
  3. Add the chicken and cook until it's no longer pink on the outside.
  4. Sprinkle in the flour and stir for a minute. Gradually add the broth, then the milk, stirring as it thickens; simmer 5 min.
  5. Stir in the green beans, corn and thyme; season lightly.
  6. Pour into a 20 cm baking dish. Lay the pastry over, trim, press the edges down and cut a few steam slits. Brush with milk.
  7. Bake 25–30 min until the pastry is puffed and golden.
👶 Little ones: chop the chicken small, and break the pastry into small pieces for the younger one.
🩸 Iron boost: chicken — pack orange or berries alongside for vitamin C.
🥡 Lunchbox: spoon filling and a piece of pastry into a warmed thermos (or pack the pastry separately so it stays crisp), with a bun and fruit. Matches Tuesday's daycare tray (Chicken Pot Pie, Buns, Fruit, Milk).

Ham & Potato Soup + Bologna Sandwich
Tuesday dinner → Wednesday lunch · 2 kid dinners + 2 kid lunches
▾
Allergen-safe (check labels)Hidden vegKid portions
Allergen-safe: The milk is simmered into the soup. Check the bologna and bread for egg and milk binders.

Soup

  • 150 g ham steak, diced iron
  • 2 medium potatoes (~400 g), diced · 60 g carrot, diced · 1 celery stalk · ½ onion, chopped
  • 15 g butter + 15 g flour · 500 ml chicken broth + 150 ml milk

Sandwiches

  • 4 slices sandwich bread, egg-free · 10 g butter
  • 60 g sliced bologna

Method

  1. Soften the onion, carrot and celery in the butter, 5 min. Stir in the flour for 1 min.
  2. Add the broth and potatoes. Simmer, covered, until the potatoes are very soft, 15 min.
  3. Mash about half the potatoes in the pot to thicken. Stir in the ham and milk and heat through without boiling hard.
  4. Sandwiches: butter the bread, fill with bologna and cut into fingers. For lunch, make them fresh Wednesday morning.
👶 Little ones: cut the bologna into small pieces; whole slices can be hard to chew.
🥡 Lunchbox: soup in a warmed thermos, sandwich fingers on the side. Matches Wednesday's daycare tray (Ham Potato Soup, Bologna Sandwich, Fruit, Milk).

Chicken & Cauliflower Macaroni
Monday Nov 9 dinner → Tuesday lunch · 2 kid dinners + 2 kid lunches
▾
Allergen-safe (cooked milk)Hidden vegKid portions
Allergen-safe: A cooked milk sauce instead of cheese. Check the macaroni isn't egg pasta.

Ingredients

  • 250 g chicken breast, in small pieces buy fresh
  • 150 g macaroni, egg-free
  • 200 g cauliflower, in small florets vit C · 40 g carrot, finely grated hidden veg
  • Sauce: 20 g butter + 20 g flour + 300 ml milk, a pinch of salt

Method

  1. Boil the macaroni, adding the cauliflower for the last 6 min. Drain.
  2. Meanwhile, cook the chicken in a little oil until no pink remains, 6–7 min. Add the carrot for the last minute.
  3. Melt the butter in the pasta pot, stir in the flour for 1 min, then whisk in the milk and simmer until thick, 3 min.
  4. Stir in the macaroni, cauliflower and chicken. Mash some of the cauliflower into the sauce for creaminess.
👶 Little ones: chop the chicken small for the younger one.
🥡 Lunchbox: reheats well with a splash of milk. Matches Tuesday's daycare tray (Chicken, Cheese, Cauliflower Macaroni, Fruit, Milk).

Rice Crispy Treats + Veggies + Milk
Make Wednesday Nov 4 → Monday Nov 9 snack · 16 squares
▾
Allergen-safe (check labels)
Allergen-safe: Just butter, marshmallows and rice cereal, with the butter melted. Check the marshmallows and cereal for a nut warning.

Ingredients

  • 30 g butter · 150 g mini marshmallows · 120 g crisp rice cereal
  • Veggies for Nov 9: ½ cucumber, as sticks
  • Milk, to drink

Method

  1. Line a 20 cm square pan with baking paper. Melt the butter in a large pot over low heat, add the marshmallows and stir until smooth.
  2. Off the heat, stir in the cereal until coated. Press firmly into the pan with a buttered spatula. Cool 1 hour.
  3. Cut into 16 squares. Freeze them in an airtight tub with baking paper between layers, and pack 2 straight from the freezer on Nov 9; they thaw by snack time.
👶 Little ones: sticky treats are hard for very young eaters. Give the younger one a small, thin piece, or skip it and send soft veggies.
🍪 Snack: matches Nov 9's daycare snack (Rice Crispy Treat, Veggie, Milk). The rest are for home.
