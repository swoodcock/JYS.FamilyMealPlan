Hot Dogs + Tossed Salad
Sunday dinner → Monday lunch · 2 kid dinners + 2 kid lunches
▾
Allergen-safe (check labels)Hidden vegKid portions
Allergen-safe: Most all-beef hot dogs are egg-free, but check the hot dogs and the buns: some wieners use egg as a binder, and some soft buns are glazed with egg. The salad dressing is just oil and vinegar, with no mayo.

Ingredients

  • 4 all-beef hot dogs, egg-free iron
  • 4 hot dog buns, egg-free
  • Salad: 80 g mixed lettuce · ½ cucumber, sliced · 100 g cherry tomatoes, quartered vit C · 40 g grated carrot · 15 g baby spinach, finely chopped hidden veg
  • Dressing, on the side: 15 ml olive oil + 5 ml red wine vinegar + a pinch of salt
  • Veggie sticks: 2 celery stalks · ½ red bell pepper vit C

Method

  1. Pan-sear or grill the hot dogs until browned all over and hot right through, 6–8 min.
  2. Toss the lettuce, cucumber, tomatoes, carrot and spinach. Keep the dressing in a separate little pot so the lunchbox salad stays crisp.
  3. Cut the celery and pepper into sticks. Warm the buns and serve.
👶 Little ones: round slices of hot dog are a choking risk. For the younger one, quarter the hot dog lengthwise and then chop it small. Halve or quarter the cherry tomatoes too.
🩸 Lighter iron night: beef and a little spinach, with tomato and red pepper for vitamin C. Spaghetti and roast beef follow with more.
🥡 Lunchbox: hot dog in its bun (or chopped, for the younger one), salad and veggie sticks in separate compartments. Matches Monday's daycare tray (Hot dogs, Tossed salad, Fruit, Milk).

Spaghetti & Meat Sauce
Monday dinner → Tuesday lunch · 2 kid dinners + 2 kid lunches
▾
Allergen-safeIron-rich ★Hidden vegKid portions
Allergen-safe: Dried spaghetti is usually just semolina and water. Check that it isn't egg pasta. The sauce has no dairy or egg. Parmesan is optional, and it melts on the hot pasta.

Ingredients

  • 160 g spaghetti, egg-free
  • 200 g ground beef iron
  • ½ onion, diced · 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 40 g carrot and ½ zucchini, finely grated · 20 g baby spinach, finely chopped hidden veg
  • 1 small can (398 ml) crushed tomatoes + 15 g tomato paste vit C
  • ½ tsp dried oregano · ½ tsp dried basil
  • Optional: 10 g grated parmesan, to serve
  • Lunchbox veggies: ½ cucumber + ½ red bell pepper, as sticks vit C

Method

  1. Brown the beef with the onion. Add the garlic, carrot, zucchini and spinach and cook 3–4 min, until the veg melts into the meat.
  2. Stir in the crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, oregano and basil. Simmer 20 min, until thick.
  3. Cook the spaghetti as the packet says, then toss with the sauce. Break the strands in half before cooking to make them easier for small hands.
🩸 Iron-rich ★: beef and spinach, with tomato and red pepper for vitamin C. The best iron night of the week.
🥡 Lunchbox: pasta and sauce together, reheated with a splash of water, veggie sticks on the side. Matches Tuesday's daycare tray (Spaghetti & meat sauce, Veggies, Fruit, Milk).

Roast Beef + Mashed Potatoes
Tuesday dinner → Wednesday lunch · 2 kid dinners + 2 kid lunches
▾
Allergen-safeIron-rich ★Kid portions
Allergen-safe: Nothing to swap. The mash uses milk and butter that are cooked in, and the gravy is thickened with cornstarch, not flour and egg.

Roast and gravy

  • 500 g beef roast (sirloin tip or eye of round) iron ★
  • 1 tsp oil · ½ tsp garlic powder · salt and pepper
  • Gravy: 150 ml chicken broth + 5 g cornstarch, plus the pan juices

Mash and sides

  • 400 g potatoes + 15 g butter + 50 ml milk
  • 100 g carrots + 100 g green beans no peas
  • 100 g broccoli vit C

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C (350°F). Rub the beef with the oil, garlic powder, salt and pepper and sear on all sides in a hot oven-safe pan.
  2. Roast about 35–40 min, until 63°C (145°F) in the centre. Rest 10 min under foil, then slice thinly across the grain.
  3. Meanwhile, boil the potatoes until tender and mash with the butter and milk. Steam the carrots, green beans and broccoli.
  4. Gravy: set the roasting pan on the stove, add the broth and scrape up the browned bits. Stir in the cornstarch, mixed with a spoonful of water, and simmer until glossy.
🩸 Iron-rich ★: beef is the strongest iron source of the week. The broccoli brings vitamin C to help absorb it.
🥡 Lunchbox: thin slices of beef with a spoonful of gravy, which keeps them moist when reheated, plus mash and veg. Cut the beef into small pieces for the younger one. Matches Wednesday's daycare tray (Roast Beef, Mashed Potatoes, Veggies, Fruit, Milk).
4
Pancit Bihon
Wednesday dinner → Thursday lunch · 2 kid dinners + 2 kid lunches
▾
Allergen-safeIron-richHidden vegKid portions
Allergen-safe: Rice vermicelli is just rice and water. Pancit is sometimes topped with sliced boiled egg, so leave the egg off. Green beans stand in for any peas.

Ingredients

  • 100 g rice vermicelli (bihon) noodles
  • 200 g boneless chicken thighs, thinly sliced iron
  • ½ onion, sliced · 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1 celery stalk, thinly sliced · 50 g carrot, cut in thin matchsticks · 50 g green beans, thinly sliced no peas
  • 15 g baby spinach, chopped iron · optional: a handful of shredded cabbage, if you have some
  • 300 ml chicken broth + 15 ml low-sodium soy sauce
  • ½ lemon, in wedges vit C

Method

  1. Soak the noodles in warm water for 10 min, until pliable. Drain and snip into shorter lengths with scissors.
  2. Soften the onion and garlic in a little oil. Add the chicken and cook through.
  3. Add the celery, carrot, green beans and cabbage, if using, and stir-fry 3–4 min. Stir in the spinach.
  4. Pour in the broth and soy sauce, add the noodles, and toss until the liquid is absorbed, 5–6 min.
  5. Squeeze over a little lemon just before serving.
🩸 Iron-rich: chicken thigh and spinach, with lemon for vitamin C.
🥡 Lunchbox: reheats well with a splash of water. Pack a lemon wedge alongside. Matches Thursday's daycare tray (Pancit Bihon, Fruit, Milk).
5
Egg-Free Chicken Fingers + Rice
Thursday dinner → Friday lunch · 2 kid dinners + 2 kid lunches
▾
Allergen-safe (egg swap)Iron boostHidden vegKid portions
Allergen-safe: Chicken fingers are normally dipped in beaten egg before breading. This uses a milk and cornstarch wash instead, which bakes on. Check the breadcrumbs are egg-free and nut-free.

Ingredients

  • 300 g chicken breast, cut into finger-sized strips iron
  • Dredge: 30 g flour + ½ tsp paprika + ½ tsp garlic powder + salt
  • Egg-free wash: 60 ml milk whisked with 8 g cornstarch
  • 60 g breadcrumbs, egg-free
  • 120 g rice (dry), with 40 g carrot and ¼ zucchini, finely diced, cooked in hidden veg
  • 100 g green beans no peas
  • Orange wedges, to serve vit C

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 190°C (375°F); it shares the oven with tonight's apple crisp. Line a tray and brush it with oil.
  2. Set out three bowls: the seasoned flour, the milk wash and the breadcrumbs. Dredge each strip in flour, dip in the wash, then press into the crumbs.
  3. Drizzle with a little oil and bake 22–25 min, turning once, until golden and 74°C (165°F) inside.
  4. Meanwhile, cook the rice with the diced carrot and zucchini stirred in. Steam the green beans.
🩸 Iron boost: chicken, with orange wedges for vitamin C.
🥡 Lunchbox: they pack and reheat well. Cut the strips into small pieces for the younger one. Matches Friday's daycare tray (Chicken Fingers, Rice, Veggies, Fruit, Milk).

Dairy-Free Yogurt + Berries
Monday snack · 2 cups
▾
Allergen-safe (dairy swap)Vit C
Allergen-safe: Cottage cheese is fresh, uncooked dairy, so this is a thick dairy-free yogurt (coconut or oat) instead. Plain cottage cheese is fine for the older one if you'd rather.

Ingredients

  • 200 g plain dairy-free yogurt (coconut or oat) — the rest of the tub goes in Friday's frozen yogurt
  • 100 g frozen berries, thawed overnight vit C
  • Water, to drink

Method

  1. Thaw the berries in a covered bowl in the fridge overnight. Chop any large ones.
  2. Spoon the yogurt into two lidded cups and top with the berries and their juice.
🍪 Snack: matches Monday's daycare snack (Cottage Cheese, Fruit, Water).

Cheese & Veggie Muffins
Tuesday snack · makes 12, freeze the extras
▾
Allergen-safe (egg swap)Hidden veg
Allergen-safe: Savoury muffins normally use egg. This batch uses a flax egg instead (10 g ground flaxseed + 45 ml water, rested 5 min). The cheese and milk are baked in.

Ingredients

  • 200 g flour · 2 tsp baking powder · ½ tsp salt · pinch of pepper
  • 1 flax egg (10 g ground flaxseed + 45 ml water)
  • 180 ml milk · 50 ml oil
  • 100 g shredded cheddar
  • ½ zucchini (~100 g) + 60 g carrot, finely grated hidden veg
  • Juice, to drink

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 190°C (375°F). Mix the flour, baking powder, salt and pepper, and mix up the flax egg.
  2. Whisk the milk, oil and flax egg, then stir into the dry ingredients until just combined.
  3. Squeeze the water out of the grated zucchini, then fold it in with the carrot and cheese.
  4. Divide between 12 muffin cups and bake 20–22 min, until golden and springy. Cool, keep 2 out, and freeze the rest.
🍪 Snack: matches Tuesday's daycare snack (Cheese and Veggie muffins, Juice). Bake Monday evening.

Grilled Cheese + Raw Veggies
Thursday snack · 2 small sandwiches
▾
Allergen-safe
Allergen-safe: Nothing to swap. The cheese is melted, so it's cooked dairy. Just check the bread is egg-free.

Ingredients

  • 4 slices sandwich bread, egg-free
  • 50 g firm cheese, sliced
  • 10 g butter, for the outside
  • Raw veggies: 2 celery stalks + 60 g carrot, as sticks
  • Water, to drink

Method

  1. Butter the outside faces of the bread and fill with the cheese.
  2. Cook in a pan over medium heat, pressing gently, until golden on both sides and the cheese has melted.
  3. Cut into fingers and pack with the veggie sticks. Best made the morning of.
👶 Little ones: raw carrot sticks are a choking risk for very young eaters. For the younger one, grate the carrot or steam the sticks for 2 min.
🍪 Snack: matches Thursday's daycare snack (Grilled cheese, Raw veggies, Water).

Dairy-Free Frozen Yogurt + Apple Crisp
Friday snack · 2 portions, plus extra crisp
▾
Allergen-safe (dairy & nut swap)High fiber
Allergen-safe: Frozen yogurt is still uncooked dairy, so this is made from dairy-free yogurt. Many crisp recipes add walnuts or pecans, so leave the nuts out of the topping. The butter in the topping is baked. It's sweetened with maple syrup, not honey, which isn't safe for babies under one.

Frozen yogurt

  • 250 g plain dairy-free yogurt — the rest of Monday's tub
  • 75 g frozen berries · 1–2 tsp maple syrup

Apple crisp (small dish)

  • Filling: 2 apples (~300 g), peeled & sliced + ½ tsp cinnamon + 15 g sugar + a squeeze of lemon vit C
  • Topping: 50 g rolled oats + 30 g flour + 30 g brown sugar + 40 g cold butter, cubed no nuts
  • Water, to drink

Method

  1. Frozen yogurt: blend the yogurt, berries and maple syrup until smooth. Freeze in a small tub at least 4 hrs. Make it early in the week; let it soften 10 min before scooping.
  2. Apple crisp: preheat oven to 190°C (375°F). Toss the apples with the cinnamon, sugar and lemon and spread in a small baking dish.
  3. Rub the butter into the oats, flour and brown sugar until crumbly, then scatter it over the apples.
  4. Bake 30–35 min, until golden and bubbling. It bakes alongside Thursday's chicken fingers.
  5. Pack the crisp in a lidded cup. Scoop the frozen yogurt into a small insulated pot in the morning.
🍪 Snack: matches Friday's daycare snack (Frozen yogurt, Apple Crisp, Water).
