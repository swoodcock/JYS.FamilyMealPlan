Spaghetti + Freezer Sauce
Sunday family dinner → Monday lunch · family + 2 kid lunches
▾
Allergen-safe (check pasta)Iron-rich (if meat sauce)FamilyLow-carb swap
Allergen-safe: Check the spaghetti isn't egg pasta. Skip cold grated parmesan on the younger one's plate; stirring a little cheddar into the hot sauce so it melts is fine.

Ingredients

  • Pasta sauce from the freezer, enough for the family + 2 kid lunches (about 1–1.2 L) iron · vit C
  • 300 g spaghetti, egg-free (kids, one adult, and lunches)
  • Low-carb swap: 2 zucchini, spiralised or cut into ribbons with a peeler
  • For Monday's lunch: ½ cucumber, as sticks · fresh fruit

Method

  1. Saturday night: move the sauce to the fridge to thaw (or defrost it in the microwave on Sunday).
  2. Boil the spaghetti until tender. Meanwhile, heat the sauce until bubbling.
  3. Low-carb swap: sauté the zucchini noodles in a little oil for 2–3 min, until just tender, and top with sauce.
  4. Set aside 2 kid portions of spaghetti and sauce for Monday's lunch before serving.
🥗 Low-carb swap: sauce over zucchini noodles instead of pasta. A side of the cucumber or a green salad bulks it out.
👶 Little ones: cut the spaghetti into 2–3 cm lengths for the younger one.
🩸 Iron: a meat sauce gives iron, and the tomato brings vitamin C.
🥡 Lunchbox: reheat until piping hot with a splash of water and pack in a warmed thermos, with cucumber sticks and fruit. Stands in for Monday's daycare tray (Tomato Macaroni Soup, Grilled Cheese, Fruit, Milk): same tomato-and-pasta idea.
2
Perogy Casserole + Broccoli
Monday kids' dinner → Tuesday lunch · 2 kid dinners + 2 kid lunches
▾
Allergen-safe (check perogies)Iron boostKids only
Allergen-safe: The milk and cheddar are baked in. Check the perogy dough is egg-free; some brands use egg. Cheese-filled perogies are fine because they're cooked.

Ingredients

  • 600 g frozen perogies (potato & cheddar)
  • 150 g ham steak, diced small iron
  • ½ onion, finely chopped · 15 g butter
  • 100 ml milk · 60 g shredded cheddar
  • 200 g broccoli, in small florets vit C

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 190°C (375°F). Boil the perogies for 3 min (they finish in the oven), then drain.
  2. Soften the onion in the butter, 5 min. Add the ham and cook for 2 min more.
  3. Toss the perogies with the ham and onion in a 20 cm baking dish. Pour the milk over, then scatter the cheddar on top.
  4. Bake 20 min, until bubbling and golden. Steam the broccoli for the last 5 min.
👶 Little ones: cut the perogies into small pieces for the younger one; the dough can be gummy.
🩸 Iron boost: ham, with broccoli for vitamin C.
🥡 Lunchbox: reheat until piping hot and pack in a warmed thermos with the broccoli. Matches Tuesday's daycare tray (Perogies Casserole, Veggies, Fruit, Milk).
3
갈비 Pie
Tuesday family dinner → Wednesday lunch · family + 2 kid lunches
▾
Allergen-safe (check sauce)Iron-rich ★FamilyLow-carb swap
Allergen-safe: A shepherd's pie made with galbi-jjim from the freezer. Before it goes in the dish: take out any chestnuts (a tree nut), and any jujube pits and ginkgo nuts. If the galbi-jjim was made with honey, the younger one shouldn't have it, even cooked, so serve them something else. Rice syrup or sugar is fine. The milk in the mash is cooked.

Filling

  • Galbi-jjim from the freezer, enough for the family + 2 kid lunches (about 1 kg with bones) iron ★
  • 100 g frozen corn
  • 10 g cornstarch + 2 tbsp cold water, to thicken

Mash topping

  • 800 g potatoes, peeled and cut into chunks
  • 30 g butter · 100 ml milk, warm · pinch of salt
  • 40 g shredded cheddar, for the top

Low-carb swap

  • 400 g cauliflower, in florets · 10 g butter

On the side

  • 200 g broccoli, steamed vit C

Method

  1. Monday morning: move the galbi-jjim to the fridge to thaw.
  2. Boil the potatoes and, in a separate pot, the cauliflower, until very soft, 15–18 min. Preheat the oven to 200°C (400°F).
  3. Meanwhile, warm the galbi-jjim. Lift out the meat, pull it off the bones and chop it small. Chop the radish and carrot from the stew small too. Pick out chestnuts, jujube pits and ginkgo.
  4. Return the meat and veg to the pot with the corn. Stir in the cornstarch slurry and simmer 2 min until the sauce is thick and glossy.
  5. Mash the potatoes with the butter, milk and salt. Mash the cauliflower with its butter.
  6. Spoon most of the filling into a 20 × 20 cm dish and a smaller one for the low-carb swap. Top the big dish with potato and the small one with cauliflower. Scatter cheddar over both.
  7. Bake 20–25 min, until golden at the edges. Steam the broccoli while it bakes.
🥗 Low-carb swap: the small dish with the cauliflower topping. Galbi-jjim sauce is sweet, so use a slotted spoon and leave most of the sauce behind for that dish.
👶 Little ones: make sure every bone fragment is out, and chop the meat finely; short rib can be chewy.
🩸 Iron-rich ★: beef short rib, with broccoli for vitamin C.
🥡 Lunchbox: reheat until piping hot and pack in a warmed thermos, with broccoli and fruit. Stands in for Wednesday's daycare tray (Shepherd's Pie, Fruit, Milk).

Frozen Pizza + Veggie Sticks
Wednesday family dinner → Thursday lunch · family + 2 kid lunches
▾
Allergen-safe (check box)FamilyLow-carb swap
Allergen-safe: The cheese is melted, so it's fine. Check the box for egg in the crust and for pesto (pine nuts). Skip pizzas with peas.

Ingredients

  • 2 frozen pizzas (one plain cheese or ham for the kids)
  • 1 cucumber + 1 bell pepper, as sticks vit C
  • Low-carb swap: 150 g baby spinach for a big salad, with some of the cucumber and pepper

Method

  1. Bake the pizzas as the box says. Cut the veggie sticks while they bake.
  2. Set aside 2 kid slices for Thursday's lunch before serving. Cool, then refrigerate.
🥗 Low-carb swap: one slice with a large spinach, cucumber and pepper salad. Thin-crust pizza is the lower-carb choice.
👶 Little ones: cut the pizza into small squares for the younger one, quarter any pepperoni, and steam the pepper sticks for 2 min.
🥡 Lunchbox: pizza cut into fingers (warm it and pack in an insulated container, or send it cold), cucumber and pepper sticks, and fruit. Stands in for Thursday's daycare tray (Chicken Tortilla Rollups, Veggies, Fruit, Milk).
5
Lazy Cabbage Roll Casserole
Thursday kids' dinner → Friday lunch · 2 kid dinners + 2 kid lunches
▾
Allergen-safeIron-rich ★Hidden vegKids only
Allergen-safe: No egg, nuts or peas. The cheddar is melted on top.

Ingredients

  • 300 g ground beef iron ★
  • ½ onion, finely chopped · 1 clove garlic, grated
  • ½ small head of green cabbage (about 350 g), finely shredded vit C · hidden veg
  • 80 g rice (dry), rinsed
  • 1 small can (398 ml) crushed tomatoes vit C · 250 ml chicken broth
  • ½ tsp paprika, a pinch of salt · 30 g shredded cheddar

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F).
  2. Brown the beef with the onion and garlic in an ovenproof pot, 6–7 min. Stir in the paprika and salt.
  3. Stir in the cabbage, raw rice, tomatoes and broth. Bring to a simmer.
  4. Cover and bake 45 min, until the rice is tender. Uncover, scatter the cheddar on top and bake 5 min more.
👶 Little ones: shred the cabbage finely so there are no long strands.
🩸 Iron-rich ★: beef, with tomato and cabbage for vitamin C.
🥡 Lunchbox: reheat until piping hot and pack in a warmed thermos, with fruit. Matches Friday's daycare tray (Lazy Cabbage Rolls, Fruit, Milk).

Banana Bread
Bake Sunday → Monday snack · 1 loaf, freeze the extra slices
▾
Allergen-safe (egg swap)
Allergen-safe: The egg-free loaf from the Oct 5 plan, with the sugar cut from 100 g to 80 g. It uses a flax egg instead of egg, and the butter is baked in. Leave out walnuts.

Ingredients

  • 3 very ripe bananas, mashed
  • 80 g butter, melted
  • 80 g brown sugar was 100 g
  • 1 flax egg (10 g ground flaxseed + 45 ml water, rested 5 min)
  • 1 tsp vanilla · 1 tsp baking soda · pinch of salt · ½ tsp cinnamon
  • 220 g flour

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 175°C (350°F); line a loaf pan.
  2. Mix the bananas, melted butter, sugar, flax egg and vanilla.
  3. Stir in the baking soda, salt and cinnamon, then fold in the flour until just combined.
  4. Bake 55–65 min until a skewer comes out clean. Cool fully before slicing.
Less sugar: the riper the bananas, the less you'll miss it. Use ones that are mostly brown.
🍪 Snack: one slice each, with water. Matches Monday's daycare snack (Banana Bread). Slice and freeze the rest.

Mini Pizza Buns + Juice
Make Monday evening → Tuesday snack · 4 halves
▾
Allergen-safe (check buns)
Allergen-safe: The mozzarella is melted. Check the buns are egg-free; some soft rolls are glazed with egg.

Ingredients

  • 2 dinner buns, egg-free, split in half
  • 20 g tomato paste, thinned with 1 tbsp water, plus a pinch of dried oregano
  • 20 g sliced deli ham, finely chopped · 40 g shredded mozzarella
  • 2 small boxes of 100% juice

Method

  1. After the perogy casserole comes out, turn the oven to 200°C (400°F).
  2. Spread the tomato on the cut halves, add the ham and mozzarella, and bake 8–10 min until the cheese bubbles.
  3. Cool, then refrigerate. Pack cold, or warm them briefly Tuesday morning.
👶 Little ones: cut the younger one's half into small squares. Juice can be watered down half and half.
🍪 Snack: matches Tuesday's daycare snack (Pizza Buns, Juice).

Banana Oat Bites + Fruit
Bake Sunday → Wednesday snack · 12 squares
▾
Allergen-safe
Allergen-safe: Daycare has Greek yogurt and granola. Store granola often has nuts and honey, so this is a soft, nut-free, honey-free oat bake held together with banana. The older one can have real Greek yogurt on the side if you like; the younger one can't have it uncooked. If you have sorbet bars left over, one of those works instead.

Ingredients

  • 1 ripe banana, mashed
  • 100 g rolled oats · 30 g butter, melted · 20 g brown sugar
  • ½ tsp cinnamon · pinch of salt
  • Fresh fruit vit C · water, to drink

Method

  1. Bake alongside the banana bread at 175°C (350°F). Line a loaf pan or small dish with baking paper.
  2. Mix everything, press firmly into the pan about 2 cm thick, and bake 20–25 min until golden at the edges.
  3. Cool fully, cut into 12 squares and freeze. Pack 2 straight from the freezer on Wednesday; they thaw by snack time.
👶 Little ones: soft enough for the younger one; break it into small pieces.
🍪 Snack: stands in for Wednesday's daycare snack (Greek Yogurt, Fruit, Granola).

Broccoli Cheddar Hand Pies
Make Thursday evening → Friday snack · 6 pies, freeze the extras
▾
Allergen-safe (milk wash)Hidden veg
Allergen-safe: The cheddar and milk are baked in. The pastry is brushed with milk, not egg. Check the pastry is egg-free.

Ingredients

  • 1 sheet puff pastry, egg-free, thawed
  • 120 g broccoli, very finely chopped vit C
  • 10 g butter + 10 g flour + 80 ml milk · 50 g shredded cheddar
  • 1 tbsp milk, for brushing — not egg

Method

  1. Thursday morning: move the pastry to the fridge to thaw.
  2. Steam the broccoli for 3 min. Melt the butter, stir in the flour for 1 min, whisk in the milk and simmer until thick. Off the heat, stir in the cheddar and broccoli. Cool 10 min.
  3. Cut the pastry into 6 squares. Put a spoonful of filling on each, fold into triangles and press the edges with a fork. Brush with milk and cut a small slit.
  4. Bake at 200°C (400°F) for 18–20 min, after the casserole comes out, until puffed and golden. Cool and refrigerate.
👶 Little ones: break the pastry into small pieces for the younger one.
🍪 Snack: one each, with water. Matches Friday's daycare snack (Broccoli Cheddar Hand Pie).
