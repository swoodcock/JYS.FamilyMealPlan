Egg-Free Chicken Fingers + Rice
Thursday dinner → Friday lunch · serves 4 + lunchbox
▾
Allergen-safe (egg swap)Iron boostHidden vegLow-carb swapAdult spiceKid favorite
Allergen-safe: Classic chicken fingers dip the strips in beaten egg before breading — this batch uses a milk + cornstarch wash instead, so no egg is needed for anyone. Check the breadcrumbs are egg-free and nut-free.

Ingredients

  • 700 g chicken breast, cut into finger-sized strips iron
  • Dredge: 80 g flour + 1 tsp paprika + 1 tsp garlic powder + salt & pepper
  • Egg-free wash: 150 ml milk whisked with 20 g cornstarch
  • 150 g breadcrumbs, egg-free
  • Oil, for the tray
  • 250 g rice (dry) — or ½ cauliflower head (~300 g) for the low-carb version
  • 200 g green beans no peas + 2 carrots hidden veg
  • Vitamin-C side: orange wedges vit C
  • Adults: sriracha or hot sauce, for dipping

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Line a tray and brush it with oil.
  2. Set out three bowls: the seasoned flour, the milk-cornstarch wash, and the breadcrumbs.
  3. Dredge each strip in flour, dip in the wash, then press firmly into the breadcrumbs.
  4. Lay on the tray, drizzle with a little oil, and bake 20–25 min, turning once, until golden and 74°C (165°F) inside.
  5. Cook the rice; steam the green beans and carrots.
  6. Serve with orange wedges, and hot sauce for the adults.
🔄 Low-carb: cauliflower rice instead of the rice; the coating is thin enough to leave as is.
🩸 Iron boost: chicken — pair with the vitamin-C orange wedges.
🥦 Hidden veg: green beans and carrots alongside, standing in for the usual peas.
🌶️ Adults: sriracha or hot sauce for dipping — pull the kids' portion first.
🥡 Lunchbox: matches Friday's daycare tray (Chicken Fingers, Rice, Veggies, Fruit, Milk). They pack and reheat well.

Cheese & Veggie Muffins
Tuesday snack · makes 12
▾
Allergen-safe (egg swap)Hidden veg
Allergen-safe: Savory muffins normally use egg — this batch uses a flax egg instead (10 g ground flaxseed + 45 ml water, rested 5 min), so it's safe for everyone. The cheese is baked in, so it's cooked dairy — fine.

Ingredients

  • 200 g flour, 2 tsp baking powder, ½ tsp salt, pepper
  • 1 flax egg (10 g ground flaxseed + 45 ml water, rested 5 min)
  • 180 ml milk, 50 ml oil
  • 100 g shredded cheese
  • 100 g grated zucchini + 60 g grated carrot hidden veg
  • Juice, to serve

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 190°C (375°F). Mix the flour, baking powder, salt and pepper.
  2. Whisk the milk, oil and flax egg; stir into the dry ingredients.
  3. Squeeze the excess water from the grated zucchini, then fold it in with the carrot and cheese.
  4. Divide into a muffin tin; bake 20–22 min until golden and springy. Best baked ahead — Monday evening for Tuesday.
  5. Pack with juice.
🥦 Hidden veg: zucchini and carrot grated straight into the batter.
🍪 Snack: matches Tuesday's daycare snack (Cheese and Veggie muffins, Juice).

Grilled Cheese + Raw Veggies
Thursday snack · serves 4
▾
Allergen-safe
Allergen-safe: Nothing to swap here — the cheese is melted, so it's cooked dairy. Just check the bread is egg-free.

Ingredients

  • 8 slices bread, egg-free
  • 120 g cheese, sliced or shredded
  • Butter, for the pan
  • Raw veggie sticks: carrot, cucumber
  • Water, to drink

Method

  1. Butter the outside faces of the bread; fill with cheese.
  2. Grill in a dry pan over medium heat, pressing gently, until golden on both sides and the cheese has melted.
  3. Cut into fingers or triangles. Pack with veggie sticks and water — best made the morning of.
🍪 Snack: matches Thursday's daycare snack (Grilled cheese, Raw veggies, Water).

Frozen Yogurt + Apple Crisp
Friday snack · serves 4–6
▾
Allergen-safe (dairy swap)
Allergen-safe: Frozen yogurt is still raw/uncooked dairy even frozen, so this batch uses dairy-free coconut or oat yogurt — safe for everyone, no separate portion. The apple crisp is naturally egg-free; leave the nuts out of the topping, as many crisp recipes add walnuts or pecans. The butter in the topping is baked, so it's cooked dairy — fine.

Ingredients

  • Frozen yogurt: 500 g dairy-free coconut or oat yogurt + 150 g berries or fruit purée + honey or maple syrup, to taste
  • Apple crisp — filling: 4 apples (~600 g), peeled & sliced + 1 tsp cinnamon + 30 g sugar + juice of ½ lemon vit C
  • Apple crisp — topping: 100 g rolled oats + 60 g flour + 60 g brown sugar + 80 g butter, cold and cubed no nuts
  • Water, to drink

Method

  1. Frozen yogurt: blend the yogurt with the fruit and a little honey; freeze in a tub at least 4 hrs. Make it early in the week — it keeps in the freezer.
  2. Apple crisp: preheat oven to 180°C (350°F). Toss the apples with cinnamon, sugar and lemon juice; spread in a baking dish.
  3. Rub the butter into the oats, flour and brown sugar until crumbly; scatter over the apples.
  4. Bake 35–40 min until the topping is golden and the fruit is bubbling. Bake Thursday evening for Friday.
  5. Pack a scoop of each with water.
🍪 Snack: matches Friday's daycare snack (Frozen yogurt, Apple Crisp, Water).
