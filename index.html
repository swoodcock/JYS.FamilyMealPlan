1
Tomato Macaroni Soup + Grilled Cheese
Sunday dinner → Monday lunch · 2 kid dinners + 2 kid lunches
▾
Allergen-safeHidden vegKid portions
Allergen-safe: Plain dried macaroni is egg-free — check the label. The splash of milk is simmered in and the sandwich cheese is melted, so both are cooked dairy.

Soup

  • 1 tsp oil · 10 g butter
  • ½ onion, 1 small carrot (~60 g) and 1 celery stalk, finely diced hidden veg
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 400 g can diced tomatoes vit C
  • 300 ml chicken broth
  • 60 g macaroni, egg-free
  • 60 ml milk · salt, pinch of sugar

Grilled cheese

  • 4 slices sandwich bread, egg-free
  • 60 g firm cheese, sliced
  • 10 g butter

Method

  1. Soften the onion, carrot and celery in the oil and butter, 6–8 min. Add the garlic for 1 min.
  2. Add the tomatoes and broth; simmer 15 min, then blend smooth so the veg disappears.
  3. Add the macaroni and simmer until tender, about 8 min, adding a splash of water if it thickens too much. Stir in the milk; season with salt and a pinch of sugar.
  4. Grilled cheese: butter the outsides, fill with cheese, and toast in a pan until golden and melted. Make Monday's pair fresh that morning so they don't go soggy.
🥦 Hidden veg: carrot and celery blended into the tomato base.
🥡 Lunchbox: soup in a thermos, sandwich wrapped separately. Matches Monday's daycare tray (Tomato Macaroni Soup, Grilled Cheese Sandwich, Fruit, Milk).
2
Perogy Casserole
Monday dinner → Tuesday lunch · 2 kid dinners + 2 kid lunches
▾
Allergen-safe (check dough)Veg sideKid portions
Allergen-safe: Check the perogie dough — some brands add egg. Perogy casseroles are usually layered with sour cream; this uses a cooked cheddar sauce instead, so there's no uncooked dairy.

Ingredients

  • 400 g frozen potato & cheese perogies, egg-free
  • 100 g cooked ham, diced iron
  • ½ onion, sliced · 10 g butter
  • Sauce: 10 g butter + 10 g flour + 150 ml milk + 50 g shredded cheddar
  • Side: 100 g green beans + 2 celery stalks, as sticks no peas

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 190°C (375°F). Boil the perogies until they float; drain.
  2. Soften the onion in the butter, then add the ham to warm through.
  3. Make the sauce: melt the butter, stir in the flour, whisk in the milk, simmer until thick, then melt in the cheddar.
  4. Toss the perogies with the onion and ham in a small baking dish, pour over the sauce, and bake 20 min until bubbling.
  5. Steam the green beans; cut the celery into sticks.
🥡 Lunchbox: matches Tuesday's daycare tray (Perogies Casserole, Veggies, Fruit, Milk).
3
Shepherd's Pie
Tuesday dinner → Wednesday lunch · 2 kid dinners + 2 kid lunches
▾
Allergen-safe (pea swap)Iron-richHidden vegKid portions
Allergen-safe: Shepherd's pie filling is classically peas and carrots — the peas are swapped for corn. The mash uses cooked milk and butter.

Ingredients

  • 230 g ground beef iron
  • ½ onion, diced · 1 small carrot (~60 g), diced hidden veg
  • 60 g frozen corn no peas
  • 10 g tomato paste · 100 ml chicken broth · pinch of thyme
  • Mash: 400 g potatoes + 15 g butter + 50 ml milk
  • 20 g shredded cheddar, to top (optional)

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Boil the potatoes until tender and mash with the butter and milk.
  2. Brown the beef with the onion and carrot. Stir in the tomato paste, broth, thyme and corn; simmer 5 min until thick.
  3. Spread in a small dish, top with the mash and cheddar, and bake 20 min until golden.
🩸 Iron-rich: beef, with tomato paste and potato bringing some vitamin C.
🥡 Lunchbox: matches Wednesday's daycare tray (Shepard Pie, Fruit, Milk).
4
Baked Chicken Tortilla Rollups
Wednesday dinner → Thursday lunch · 2 kid dinners + 2 kid lunches
▾
Allergen-safe (dairy swap)Raw veg sideKid portions
Allergen-safe: Tortilla rollups are usually held together with cream cheese, which is uncooked dairy. These are baked instead, so melted cheddar does the job. Check the tortillas are egg-free.

Ingredients

  • 200 g chicken breast
  • 4 small flour tortillas, egg-free
  • 50 g shredded cheddar
  • ½ bell pepper, finely diced vit C
  • ½ tsp mild taco seasoning · 1 tsp oil
  • Veggies: 2 celery stalks + ½ cucumber, as sticks

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Poach or pan-cook the chicken until cooked through, then shred.
  2. Toss the chicken with the pepper and taco seasoning.
  3. Fill each tortilla with chicken and cheese, roll tightly, and lay seam-side down on a lined tray. Brush with oil.
  4. Bake 12–15 min until crisp and the cheese has melted. Cool, then halve.
🥡 Lunchbox: pack with the veggie sticks. Matches Thursday's daycare tray (Chicken Tortilla Rollups, Veggies, Fruit, Milk).
5
Lazy Cabbage Rolls
Thursday dinner → Friday lunch · 2 kid dinners + 2 kid lunches
▾
Allergen-safeIron-richHidden vegKid portions
Allergen-safe: Nothing to swap — beef, rice, cabbage and tomato, with no egg or dairy at all.

Ingredients

  • 230 g ground beef iron
  • ½ onion, diced · 1 clove garlic
  • 300 g cabbage (about ¼ head), shredded hidden veg
  • 70 g rice (dry)
  • 400 g can diced tomatoes + 200 ml chicken broth vit C
  • Salt, pepper, ½ tsp paprika

Method

  1. Brown the beef with the onion and garlic.
  2. Add the cabbage and cook until it starts to wilt, about 5 min.
  3. Stir in the rice, tomatoes, broth and paprika. Cover and simmer 20–25 min, stirring now and then, until the rice is tender.
🩸 Iron-rich: beef, with tomato and cabbage for vitamin C.
🥡 Lunchbox: matches Friday's daycare tray (Lazy Cabbage Rolls, Fruit, Milk).

Banana Bread
Monday snack · 1 loaf, freeze the extra slices
▾
Allergen-safe (egg swap)
Allergen-safe: Uses a flax egg instead of egg (10 g ground flaxseed + 45 ml water, rested 5 min). Many banana bread recipes add walnuts — leave them out.

Ingredients

  • 3 very ripe bananas, mashed
  • 80 g butter, melted
  • 100 g brown sugar
  • 1 flax egg (10 g ground flaxseed + 45 ml water, rested 5 min)
  • 1 tsp vanilla · 1 tsp baking soda · pinch of salt · ½ tsp cinnamon
  • 220 g flour

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 175°C (350°F); line a loaf pan.
  2. Mix the bananas, melted butter, sugar, flax egg and vanilla.
  3. Stir in the baking soda, salt and cinnamon, then fold in the flour until just combined.
  4. Bake 55–65 min until a skewer comes out clean. Cool fully before slicing.
🍪 Snack: matches Monday's daycare snack (Banana bread, Water). Bake Sunday.

Pizza Buns
Tuesday snack · 4 halves
▾
Allergen-safe (check labels)
Allergen-safe: Check the buns are egg-free — some soft buns are glazed with egg. The mozzarella is baked on.

Ingredients

  • 2 hamburger buns, egg-free, split
  • 4 tbsp pizza sauce or marinara
  • 60 g shredded mozzarella
  • A few slices of pepperoni or diced ham (optional)
  • Juice, to drink

Method

  1. Spread sauce on each bun half, top with cheese and pepperoni.
  2. Bake or toaster-oven at 200°C (400°F) 6–8 min until the cheese melts.
  3. Cool, wrap, and pack with juice. Make Monday evening or Tuesday morning.
🍪 Snack: matches Tuesday's daycare snack (Pizza Buns, Juice).

Broccoli Cheddar Hand Pies
Friday snack · makes 6, freeze the extras
▾
Allergen-safe (egg swap)Hidden veg
Allergen-safe: Hand pies are normally sealed and glazed with beaten egg — these are brushed with milk instead. Check the pastry itself is egg-free. The cheese is baked inside.

Ingredients

  • 1 sheet puff pastry, egg-free, thawed
  • 150 g broccoli, finely chopped and steamed hidden veg
  • 60 g shredded cheddar
  • Binder: 5 g butter + 5 g flour + 40 ml milk, cooked to a thick sauce
  • 1 tbsp milk, for brushing — not egg

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Make the thick sauce, then stir in the broccoli and cheddar; let it cool.
  2. Cut the pastry into 6 squares. Spoon filling onto one half of each, fold over and press the edges with a fork.
  3. Cut a small slit in each, brush with milk, and bake 18–20 min until puffed and golden.
🍪 Snack: matches Friday's daycare snack (Broccoli Cheddar Hand Pie, Water). Bake Thursday evening.
