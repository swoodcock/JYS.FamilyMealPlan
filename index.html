Hot Dogs + Tossed Salad
Sunday dinner → Monday lunch · 2 kid dinners + 2 kid lunches
▾
Allergen-safe (check labels)Hidden vegKid portions
Allergen-safe: Most all-beef hot dogs are egg-free, but check the hot dogs and the buns: some wieners use egg as a binder, and some soft buns are glazed with egg. The salad dressing is just oil and vinegar, with no mayo.

Ingredients

  • 4 all-beef hot dogs, egg-free iron
  • 4 hot dog buns, egg-free
  • Salad: 80 g mixed lettuce · ½ cucumber, sliced · 100 g cherry tomatoes, quartered vit C · 40 g grated carrot · 15 g baby spinach, finely chopped hidden veg
  • Dressing, on the side: 15 ml olive oil + 5 ml red wine vinegar + a pinch of salt
  • Veggie sticks: 2 celery stalks · ½ red bell pepper vit C

Method

  1. Pan-sear or grill the hot dogs until browned all over and hot right through, 6–8 min.
  2. Toss the lettuce, cucumber, tomatoes, carrot and spinach. Keep the dressing in a separate little pot so the lunchbox salad stays crisp.
  3. Cut the celery and pepper into sticks. Warm the buns and serve.
👶 Little ones: round slices of hot dog are a choking risk. For the younger one, quarter the hot dog lengthwise and then chop it small. Halve or quarter the cherry tomatoes too.
🩸 Lighter iron night: beef and a little spinach, with tomato and red pepper for vitamin C. Spaghetti and roast beef follow with more.
🥡 Lunchbox: hot dog in its bun (or chopped, for the younger one), salad and veggie sticks in separate compartments. Matches Monday's daycare tray (Hot dogs, Tossed salad, Fruit, Milk).

Spaghetti & Meat Sauce
Monday dinner → Tuesday lunch · 2 kid dinners + 2 kid lunches + 2 parent dinners
▾
Allergen-safe (check pasta)Iron-richLow-carb swapFamily
Allergen-safe: Dried spaghetti is usually just semolina and water. Check that it isn't egg pasta. No parmesan on the kids' plates.

Ingredients

  • Your meat sauce from the freezer, enough for 6 portions iron
  • 360 g spaghetti, egg-free (160 g for the kids, 200 g for the parents)
  • Low-carb swap: 1 zucchini, cut into ribbons with a peeler
  • Lunchbox veggies: ½ cucumber + ½ red bell pepper, as sticks vit C

Method

  1. Thaw the sauce in the fridge from Sunday night. Reheat it in a covered pot until bubbling right through.
  2. Cook the spaghetti as the packet says. Break the kids' share in half before cooking so it's easier for small hands.
  3. Toss the kids' pasta with sauce and set aside 2 lunch portions before serving.
🔄 Low-carb: a half portion of pasta mixed with zucchini ribbons. Drop the ribbons into the pasta water for the last minute.
🩸 Iron-rich: the beef in the sauce, with red pepper sticks for vitamin C.
🥡 Lunchbox: pasta and sauce together, reheated with a splash of water, veggie sticks on the side. Matches Tuesday's daycare tray (Spaghetti & meat sauce, Veggies, Fruit, Milk).

Galbi + Rice
Tuesday kids' dinner → Wednesday lunch · 2 kid dinners + 2 kid lunches
▾
Allergen-safe (check marinade)Iron-rich ★Kid portions
Allergen-safe: No dairy or egg. If the galbi is store-bought, check the marinade for pine nuts, a tree nut sometimes used in Korean marinades. Honey isn't recommended under age one, even cooked, so check for that too if the younger one is under one.

Ingredients

  • About 500 g frozen galbi (marinated short ribs), from the freezer iron ★
  • 120 g rice (dry)
  • 150 g broccoli vit C + 100 g carrots, steamed

Method

  1. Move the galbi to the fridge on Monday night to thaw.
  2. Start the rice. Sear the galbi in a very hot pan or under the broiler, 3–4 min a side, until caramelised and cooked through.
  3. Steam the broccoli and carrots. Cut the meat off the bones and into bite-size pieces.
👶 Little ones: take all the meat off the bone and cut it small for the younger one. Galbi can be chewy.
🩸 Iron-rich ★: beef is the strongest iron source of the week, with broccoli for vitamin C.
🥡 Lunchbox: beef pieces over rice with the veg. A spoonful of water before reheating keeps the rice soft. Stands in for Wednesday's daycare tray (Roast Beef, Mashed Potatoes, Veggies, Fruit, Milk).

Bulgogi with Mushrooms
Tuesday parents' dinner · 2 parent dinners + leftovers for Wednesday
▾
ParentsLow-carb swapAdult spiceIron-rich

Ingredients

  • About 600 g frozen bulgogi, from the freezer iron
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 200 g mushrooms, sliced
  • 3 green onions, cut in 3 cm lengths (the rest of the bunch goes in Wednesday's pancit)
  • Rice from the kids' pot, or the low-carb swap below
  • Optional heat: gochujang or chili flakes

Method

  1. Move the bulgogi to the fridge on Monday night to thaw.
  2. Fry the onion and mushrooms in a little oil over high heat until browned, 4–5 min.
  3. Add the bulgogi and stir-fry until cooked and the marinade turns sticky, 5–6 min. Toss in the green onions for the last minute.
  4. Set aside about 250 g in a tub for Wednesday's pancit before serving.
🔄 Low-carb: skip the rice and serve over a big scoop of the kids' steamed broccoli, or wrapped in lettuce leaves.
🌶️ Adult spice: a spoon of gochujang or chili flakes at the end.

Bulgogi Pancit
Wednesday dinner → Thursday lunch · 2 kid dinners + 2 kid lunches + 2 parent dinners
▾
Allergen-safeIron-richHidden vegLow-carb swapAdult spiceFamily
Allergen-safe: Rice vermicelli is just rice and water. Pancit is sometimes topped with sliced boiled egg, so leave the egg off. Green beans stand in for any peas.

Ingredients

  • 200 g rice vermicelli (bihon) noodles
  • About 250 g leftover bulgogi, with its onions and mushrooms, sliced thinly iron
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 celery stalks, thinly sliced · 100 g carrot, in thin matchsticks · 100 g green beans, thinly sliced no peas
  • 30 g baby spinach, chopped hidden veg
  • 3 green onions, sliced
  • 450 ml chicken broth + 15 ml low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 lemon, in wedges vit C

Method

  1. Soak the noodles in warm water for 10 min, until pliable. Drain and snip into shorter lengths with scissors.
  2. Soften the garlic in a little oil. Add the celery, carrot and green beans and stir-fry 3–4 min.
  3. Add the bulgogi and stir-fry 1–2 min to heat it through. Stir in the spinach.
  4. Pour in the broth and soy sauce, add the noodles, and toss until the liquid is absorbed, 5–6 min. Scatter over the green onions.
  5. Pull out the kids' 4 portions, then add any heat to the rest. Squeeze over lemon just before serving.
🔄 Low-carb: serve that portion with fewer noodles and more of the beef and veg from the pan.
🌶️ Adult spice: chili flakes or a little gochujang, after the kids' portions are out.
🩸 Iron-rich: beef and spinach, with lemon for vitamin C.
🥡 Lunchbox: reheats well with a splash of water. Cut the beef small for the younger one and pack a lemon wedge alongside. Matches Thursday's daycare tray (Pancit Bihon, Fruit, Milk).

Chicken Strips + Rice
Thursday dinner → Friday lunch · 2 kid dinners + 2 kid lunches + 2 parent dinners
▾
Allergen-safe (check box)Iron boostHidden vegLow-carb swapFamily
Allergen-safe: Many frozen breaded chicken strips use egg in the batter or breading. Check the box for egg and for a nut warning. Milk in the breading is fine once baked.

Ingredients

  • Frozen premade chicken strips, egg-free — enough for 6 portions (about 700 g) iron
  • 220 g rice (dry), with 40 g carrot and ¼ zucchini, finely diced, cooked in hidden veg
  • 250 g green beans no peas
  • Orange wedges, to serve vit C

Method

  1. Bake the strips as the box says, on two trays if needed. The apple crisp can share the oven: bake it at the box temperature and check it from 25 min.
  2. Meanwhile, cook the rice with the diced carrot and zucchini stirred in. Steam the green beans.
🔄 Low-carb: skip the rice and double up on green beans.
🩸 Iron boost: chicken, with orange wedges for vitamin C.
🥡 Lunchbox: they pack and reheat well. Cut the strips into small pieces for the younger one. Matches Friday's daycare tray (Chicken Fingers, Rice, Veggies, Fruit, Milk).

Fruit Sorbet Bar + Apple Crisp
Friday snack · 2 portions, plus extra crisp
▾
Allergen-safe (check label)High fiber
Allergen-safe: Check the box says sorbet, not sherbet. Sherbet contains milk. Check for a nut warning too. Many crisp recipes add walnuts or pecans, so leave the nuts out of the topping. The butter in the topping is baked.

Sorbet bar

  • 2 store-bought fruit sorbet bars, dairy-free

Apple crisp (small dish)

  • Filling: 2 apples (~300 g), peeled & sliced + ½ tsp cinnamon + 15 g sugar + a squeeze of lemon vit C
  • Topping: 50 g rolled oats + 30 g flour + 30 g brown sugar + 40 g cold butter, cubed no nuts
  • Water, to drink

Method

  1. Apple crisp: preheat oven to 190°C (375°F). Toss the apples with the cinnamon, sugar and lemon and spread in a small baking dish.
  2. Rub the butter into the oats, flour and brown sugar until crumbly, then scatter it over the apples.
  3. Bake 30–35 min, until golden and bubbling. It bakes alongside Thursday's chicken strips.
  4. Pack the crisp in a lidded cup. Send the sorbet bar in an insulated bag with an ice pack, or ask the daycare to keep it in their freezer until snack time.
🍪 Snack: stands in for Friday's daycare snack (Frozen yogurt, Apple Crisp, Water).
