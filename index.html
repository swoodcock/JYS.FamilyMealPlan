Tater Tot Casserole
Sunday dinner → Monday lunch · serves 4 + lunchbox
▾
Allergen-safeLow-carb swapHidden vegIron-rich
Allergen-safe: Frozen tater tots are naturally egg-free (potato + starch). Cheese on top is cooked/melted — fine.

Ingredients

  • 600 g ground beef iron
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 200 g green beans no peas + 150 g corn kernels hidden veg
  • Sauce: 30 g butter + 30 g flour + 300 ml beef broth + 150 ml milk
  • 700 g frozen tater tots
  • 150 g shredded cheddar
  • Vitamin-C side: orange wedges vit C

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Brown the beef with onion; drain excess fat.
  2. Stir in the green beans and corn.
  3. Make the gravy: melt butter, whisk in flour, add broth and milk, simmer until thickened. Combine with the beef mixture and spread in a baking dish.
  4. Top with a single layer of tater tots, then the cheese.
  5. Bake 25–30 min until the tots are golden and the casserole is bubbling. Serve with orange wedges.
🔄 Low-carb: skip the tots and top with roasted cauliflower florets instead.
🩸 Iron: beef — pair with the vitamin-C orange wedges.
🥦 Hidden veg: green beans and corn folded right into the beef.
🥡 Lunchbox: matches Monday's daycare tray (Tator Tot Casserole, Veggies, Fruit, Milk).

Mexican Lasagna
Monday dinner → Tuesday lunch · serves 4 + lunchbox
▾
Allergen-safeIron-richHidden vegLow-carb swapAdult spice
Allergen-safe: Built with corn tortillas instead of lasagna noodles, so it's naturally egg-free. Cheese is cooked/melted — fine.

Ingredients

  • 600 g ground beef iron + 1 tbsp taco seasoning
  • 1 onion, diced · 1 bell pepper, diced · 60 g baby spinach, chopped hidden veg
  • 400 g can black beans, drained & rinsed iron + fiber
  • 400 g can diced tomatoes vit C
  • 10–12 small corn tortillas, egg-free
  • 200 g shredded cheese
  • Adults: jalapeños, hot sauce

Method

  1. Brown the beef with onion, pepper and spinach. Add taco seasoning, tomatoes and black beans; simmer 10 min.
  2. Layer in a baking dish: tortillas, meat mixture, cheese — repeat 3 times, finishing with cheese on top.
  3. Bake 190°C (375°F) 20 min until bubbly. Rest 5 min, then slice.
🔄 Low-carb: use fewer tortilla layers, bulked out with extra spinach and peppers instead.
🩸 Iron-rich: beef + black beans + spinach; tomatoes bring vitamin C.
🌶️ Adults: jalapeños or hot sauce at the table.
🥡 Lunchbox: matches Tuesday's daycare tray (Mexican Lasagna, Fruit, Milk).

Chicken Alfredo (egg-free pasta)
Tuesday dinner → Wednesday lunch · serves 4 + lunchbox
▾
Allergen-safe (pasta swap)Iron-richHidden vegLow-carb swap
Allergen-safe: Traditional cheese tortellini's fresh pasta dough almost always contains egg — we use a dried egg-free pasta shape instead, so no substitution is needed for anyone. The Alfredo sauce is cooked cream — fine.

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken breast, diced protein
  • 250 g egg-free pasta (penne or fusilli, standing in for tortellini)
  • Sauce: 40 g butter + 300 ml heavy cream + 80 g grated parmesan + 1 clove garlic
  • 300 g broccoli florets vit C · 60 g baby spinach iron

Method

  1. Cook the pasta to package instructions; drain.
  2. Sear the diced chicken until cooked through.
  3. Make the sauce: melt butter, add cream and garlic, simmer 3–4 min, then whisk in parmesan until smooth.
  4. Steam the broccoli. Toss pasta, chicken, sauce, broccoli and spinach together until the spinach wilts.
🔄 Low-carb: swap the pasta for zucchini noodles (2 zucchini, spiralized).
🩸 Iron-rich: chicken + spinach; broccoli brings vitamin C.
🥡 Lunchbox: matches Wednesday's daycare tray (Tortellini with Chicken & Alfredo Sauce, Veggies, Fruit, Milk) — egg-free pasta standing in for the tortellini.

Bean & Sausage Stew + Buns
Wednesday dinner → Thursday lunch · serves 4 + lunchbox
▾
Allergen-safeIron-richHidden vegLow-carb swap
Allergen-safe: Naturally egg-free — check the sausage label to be sure. No dairy in the stew itself.

Ingredients

  • 500 g smoked sausage, egg-free, sliced iron
  • 2 × 400 g cans white beans (navy or cannellini), drained iron + fiber
  • 1 onion, diced · 2 carrots, diced · 1 celery stalk, diced hidden veg
  • 400 g can diced tomatoes vit C
  • 800 ml chicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 bay leaf, 1 tsp dried thyme
  • Buns, egg-free, to serve

Method

  1. Sauté onion, carrot and celery. Add sausage; brown lightly.
  2. Add beans, tomatoes, broth, bay leaf and thyme. Simmer 25–30 min until slightly thickened.
  3. Discard the bay leaf. Serve with buns.
🔄 Low-carb: serve over sautéed greens instead of a bun, or use a smaller bun portion.
🩸 Iron-rich: sausage + white beans; tomatoes bring vitamin C.
🥡 Lunchbox: matches Thursday's daycare tray (Bean Stew, Buns, Fruit, Milk).

Meatloaf + Mashed Potatoes
Thursday dinner → Friday lunch · serves 4 + lunchbox
▾
Allergen-safe (egg swap)Iron-richHidden vegLow-carb swapAdult spice
Allergen-safe: Classic meatloaf binds with egg — this batch uses a flax egg instead (10 g ground flaxseed + 45 ml water, rested 5 min), so it's safe for everyone. Mash uses cooked milk and butter — fine.

Ingredients

  • 700 g ground beef iron
  • 1 flax egg (10 g ground flaxseed + 45 ml water, rested 5 min)
  • 60 g breadcrumbs, egg-free
  • 1 onion, finely diced · 60 g grated carrot hidden veg
  • 60 g ketchup, for the glaze
  • Mash: 900 g potatoes + butter + milk — plus half a cauliflower head (~300 g) for the low-carb mash
  • Steamed green beans no peas
  • Vitamin-C side: apple sauce vit C

Method

  1. Mix beef, flax egg, breadcrumbs, onion, carrot, salt and pepper. Form into a loaf in a baking dish; spread the ketchup glaze on top.
  2. Bake 180°C (350°F) 45–50 min until internal temperature reaches 71°C (160°F). Rest 10 min, then slice.
  3. Boil the potatoes (and cauliflower separately); mash each with butter and milk.
  4. Steam the green beans. Serve with apple sauce.
🔄 Low-carb: cauliflower mash instead of potato.
🩸 Iron-rich: beef — pair with the vitamin-C apple sauce.
🌶️ Adults: stir extra hot sauce into the glaze at the table.
🥡 Lunchbox: matches Friday's daycare tray (Meatloaf, Mashed Potatoes, Veggies, Fruit, Milk).

Bonus: Japchae (Korean Glass Noodles)
Friday dinner (weekend, no lunch to match) · serves 4
▾
Allergen-safeIron-richHidden vegLow-carb swapAdult spiceKid favorite
Allergen-safe: Naturally egg-free and dairy-free. Traditional egg-strip garnish is skipped, so no separate portion is needed.

Ingredients

  • 250 g sweet potato glass noodles (dangmyeon)
  • 400 g beef, thinly sliced (ribeye or sirloin) iron
  • Marinade/sauce: 60 ml soy sauce, 30 ml sesame oil, 20 g sugar, 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 onion, sliced · 2 carrots, julienned · 1 red bell pepper, julienned vit C
  • 150 g mushrooms (shiitake or button), sliced
  • 100 g baby spinach hidden veg
  • Sesame seeds, to finish
  • Adults: gochugaru or chili oil

Method

  1. Marinate the sliced beef in half the soy/sesame/sugar/garlic mixture for 15 min.
  2. Cook the noodles per package instructions; drain and toss with a little sesame oil so they don't stick.
  3. Stir-fry the onion, carrot, mushroom and pepper until just tender; add the spinach at the end to wilt. Set aside.
  4. Stir-fry the beef until browned. Combine noodles, vegetables, beef and the remaining sauce; toss well over heat 2–3 min.
  5. Top with sesame seeds. Adults add gochugaru or chili oil at the table.
🔄 Low-carb: use a smaller noodle portion, bulked out with extra vegetables.
🩸 Iron-rich: beef + spinach; bell pepper brings vitamin C.
🌶️ Adults: gochugaru or chili oil at the table.
🎉 Free Friday: nothing to pack for the weekend.

Chocolate Chip Cookies
Monday snack · makes ~18
▾
Allergen-safe (egg swap)
Allergen-safe: Standard cookies use egg — this batch uses a flax egg instead (10 g ground flaxseed + 45 ml water, rested 5 min), so it's safe for everyone. Check the chocolate chips are nut-free.

Ingredients

  • 200 g flour
  • 100 g butter, softened
  • 100 g brown sugar, 50 g granulated sugar
  • 1 flax egg (10 g ground flaxseed + 45 ml water, rested 5 min)
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 150 g chocolate chips, nut-free
  • Fresh fruit and milk, to serve

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C (350°F). Cream the butter and sugars; mix in the flax egg.
  2. Fold in the flour and baking soda, then the chocolate chips.
  3. Scoop onto a lined tray; bake 10–12 min until just set. Cool before storing.
  4. Serve with fresh fruit and milk.
🍪 Snack: matches Monday's daycare snack (Cookies, Fruit, Milk).

Apple Cinnamon Muffins + Cheese

Allergen-safe (egg swap)Hidden fruit
Allergen-safe: Standard muffins use egg — this batch uses a flax egg instead (10 g ground flaxseed + 45 ml water, rested 5 min), so it's safe for everyone. Cheese cubes are cooked/not raw dairy — fine.

Ingredients

  • 200 g flour
  • 1 apple, grated
  • 1 tsp cinnamon, 1 tsp baking powder
  • 60 ml milk
  • 1 flax egg (10 g ground flaxseed + 45 ml water, rested 5 min)

Ingredients

  3. Pack both with veggie sticks and milk.

Soft Breakfast Rolls
Wednesday breakfast · makes 12
▾
Allergen-safe (egg swap)
Allergen-safe: Yeasted breakfast rolls often use egg in the dough or an egg wash — this batch uses a flax egg instead (10 g ground flaxseed + 45 ml water, rested 5 min), so it's safe for everyone.

Ingredients

  • 400 g bread flour, 7 g instant yeast, 40 g sugar, ½ tsp salt
  • 200 ml warm milk, 40 g melted butter
  • 1 flax egg (10 g ground flaxseed + 45 ml water, rested 5 min)
  • Juice, to serve

Method

  1. Mix flour, yeast, sugar and salt. Add warm milk, melted butter and the flax egg; knead into a soft dough. Rise 1 hr.
  2. Divide into 12 rolls; place on a lined tray. Rise 30 min more.
  3. Bake 180°C (350°F) 15–18 min until golden. Best made ahead — bake Tuesday evening for Wednesday morning.
  4. Serve warm with juice.
🌅 Breakfast: matches Wednesday's daycare breakfast (Breakfast Rolls, Juice).

Sugar-Free Pudding + Animal Crackers
Thursday snack · serves 4
▾
Allergen-safe
Allergen-safe: Naturally egg-free — cornstarch-thickened, not a custard. Cooked milk is fine, no raw dairy.

Ingredients

  • 500 ml milk
  • 30 g cornstarch
  • Sugar substitute (stevia or erythritol), to taste
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Animal crackers, egg-free
  • Fresh fruit and water, to serve

Method

  1. Whisk milk, cornstarch and sweetener in a saucepan.
  2. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until thickened.
  3. Off heat, stir in vanilla. Chill at least 2 hrs — make Wednesday evening for Thursday's snack.
  4. Pack with animal crackers, fruit and water.
🍪 Snack: matches Thursday's daycare snack (Sugar Free Pudding, Animal Crackers, Fruit, Water).
