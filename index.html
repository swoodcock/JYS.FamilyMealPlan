Beef Patties + Rice (premade)
Sunday dinner → Monday lunch · serves 4 + lunchbox

Allergen-safe (check label)Low-carb swap
Allergen-safe: This is a premade night — check the frozen patties' label for egg, dairy, tree nut, peanut, or pea content before serving.

Ingredients

  • ~700 g frozen premade beef patties iron
  • Rice for the family (instant/frozen pouches or home-cooked) — plus cauliflower rice for the low-carb portion

Chicken Pot Pie + Buns
Monday dinner → Tuesday lunch · serves 4 + lunchbox
▾
Allergen-safeIron boostHidden vegLow-carb swapAdult spice
Allergen-safe: Use an egg-free pie crust/puff pastry (check the label) and brush the top with milk, not egg wash. The creamy filling is cooked milk (fine). Check buns are egg-wash-free. No nuts, no peas — corn + green beans instead.

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken breast or thigh, diced protein
  • 1 onion, diced · 2 carrots, grated/diced · 1 celery stalk, diced hidden veg
  • 100 g corn · 100 g green beans, chopped no peas
  • 60 g baby spinach, finely chopped iron
  • Sauce: 30 g butter + 30 g flour + 500 ml chicken broth or milk
  • 2 sheets egg-free pie crust or puff pastry check label
  • Soft dinner rolls/buns, egg-wash-free
  • Salt, pepper, ½ tsp dried thyme · Adults: black pepper / hot sauce

Method

  1. Sear chicken with onion. Add carrot, celery, green beans, corn, spinach; cook 4 min.
  2. Make the roux: melt butter, whisk in flour, add broth/milk, simmer to a thick sauce; fold through the chicken and veg.
  3. Pour into a baking dish, top with pastry (brush with milk), bake 200°C (400°F) 25–30 min until golden.
  4. Warm the buns alongside.
🔄 Low-carb: skip the pastry top — serve the filling over mashed cauliflower instead.
🩸 Iron boost: chicken is moderate iron; the chopped spinach lifts it — pair with a vitamin-C side.
🥦 Hidden veg: carrot, celery, corn and spinach folded into the creamy filling.
🌶️ Adults: black pepper or hot sauce at the table.
🥡 Lunchbox: filling + a torn bun reheats well, matching Tuesday's daycare tray.

Ham & Potato Soup + Sandwiches
Tuesday dinner → Wednesday lunch · serves 4 + lunchbox
▾
Allergen-safeIron boostHidden vegLow-carb swap
Allergen-safe: Soup uses cooked milk (fine). Check bread & bologna are egg-free and nut-free; use egg-free mayo or mustard on the allergen-safe sandwich, or skip the spread.

Ingredients

  • 300 g cooked ham, diced protein
  • 700 g potatoes, diced · 1 onion, diced · 2 carrots, diced · 1 celery stalk, diced hidden veg
  • 60 g baby spinach, chopped iron
  • 500 ml milk + 500 ml chicken or vegetable broth
  • 20 g butter + 20 g flour (roux)
  • Bread + sliced bologna (egg-free, nut-free) · egg-free mayo or mustard

Method

  1. Sauté onion, carrot and celery. Add potatoes and broth; simmer 15 min until tender.
  2. Stir in ham, the roux (butter + flour + milk), and spinach; simmer 5 min until thickened.
  3. Assemble bologna sandwiches on the side.
🔄 Low-carb: skip the sandwich bread and serve a smaller potato portion in the soup, or bulk the bowl with extra veg instead.
🩸 Iron boost: ham + spinach; pair with a vitamin-C fruit side.
🥦 Hidden veg: carrot, celery and spinach blended into the creamy broth.
🥡 Lunchbox: soup in a thermos + sandwich, matching Wednesday's daycare tray.

Pizza Night + Caesar Salad
Wednesday dinner → Thursday lunch · serves 4 + lunchbox
▾
Allergen-safeIron-richHidden vegLow-carb swapAdult spice
Allergen-safe: Check the dough is egg-free. Traditional Caesar dressing uses raw egg — use a pasteurized/egg-free Caesar dressing (or a yogurt-based version) for the allergen-safe portion. Parmesan (hard, aged) is fine as a topping.

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough, egg-free check label
  • 400 g pizza sauce · 300 g mozzarella
  • 300 g ground beef, browned, or pepperoni iron
  • Toppings: mushrooms, bell peppers, finely diced spinach hidden in the sauce hidden veg
  • Caesar salad: romaine lettuce, egg-free Caesar dressing, parmesan, egg-free croutons
  • Adults: chili flakes, hot honey

Method

  1. Roll out the dough, spread with sauce, top with cheese, beef and vegetables.
  2. Bake at 220°C (425°F) 10–12 min until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbling.
  3. Toss romaine with the egg-free Caesar dressing and parmesan; add croutons.
🔄 Low-carb: cauliflower crust, or skip the crust and serve the toppings over the Caesar salad instead; skip the croutons.
🩸 Iron-rich: ground beef + spinach hidden in the sauce, with the tomato sauce for vitamin C.
🌶️ Adults: chili flakes or a hot honey drizzle after baking.
🥡 Lunchbox: a slice + a small salad portion, matching Thursday's daycare tray.

Zucchini Carrot Avocado Muffins
Tuesday snack · makes 12
▾
Allergen-safe (egg swap)Hidden veg
Allergen-safe: The original recipe uses 2 eggs → for the egg-free batch, swap in 2 flax eggs (20 g ground flaxseed + 90 ml water, rested 5 min). The Greek yogurt bakes in = cooked dairy, which is fine.

Ingredients

  • 215 g white whole wheat flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder, ½ tsp baking soda, 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 150 g avocado (1 medium)
  • 2 eggs, or 2 flax eggs (20 g ground flaxseed + 90 ml water) for the egg-free batch
  • 115 g plain Greek yogurt
  • 130 g banana (1 medium)
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 60 g grated zucchini, squeezed · 45 g grated carrot, squeezed hidden veg

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 190°C (375°F).
  2. Mix the dry ingredients in a large bowl.
  3. Blend the avocado, eggs (or flax eggs), yogurt, banana and vanilla until smooth; stir into the dry mixture.
  4. Gently fold in the zucchini and carrot — don't overmix.
  5. Divide into a 12-cup muffin tin; bake 20–22 min until a toothpick comes out clean.
  6. Cool 3–5 min before turning out onto a rack.
🥦 Hidden veg: zucchini and carrot fold in barely noticeable against the banana/avocado sweetness.
🍪 Snack: pack 1–2 to match Tuesday's daycare snack (Ham, Cheese, Zucchini Muffin, Water).

Apple Cinnamon Muffins
Friday snack · makes 12
▾
Allergen-safe
Allergen-safe: Already egg-free as written. Butter, sour cream and milk bake in = cooked dairy, which is fine.

Ingredients

  • 240 g all-purpose flour
  • 1½ tsp baking powder, ¼ tsp baking soda, ½ tsp salt, ½ tsp cinnamon, pinch nutmeg
  • 114 g unsalted butter, melted
  • 100 g brown sugar, 50 g granulated sugar
  • 120 g sour cream or plain yogurt
  • 120 ml milk
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 200 g apple, peeled and chopped
  • Topping: 42 g melted butter, 3 tbsp granulated sugar, 1½ tsp cinnamon

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 220°C (425°F). Grease or line a muffin tin.
  2. Whisk flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt, cinnamon and nutmeg.
  3. Whisk melted butter, sour cream, both sugars and vanilla until smooth; stir in the milk.
  4. Fold the dry ingredients into the wet; fold in the apple — don't overmix.
  5. Divide into the tin, filling to the top. Bake 5 min at 220°C (425°F), then reduce to 175°C (350°F) and bake 14–15 min more until a tester comes out clean.
  6. Cool 5 min in the pan, then transfer to a rack.
  7. Mix the sugar-cinnamon topping; brush warm muffin tops with melted butter, then roll in the topping.
🍪 Snack: pack 1–2 to match Friday's daycare snack (Apple Salsa Cinnamon Crisps, Milk).
