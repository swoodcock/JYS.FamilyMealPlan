Beef & Bean Chili with Cheese
Sunday dinner → Monday lunch · serves 4 + lunchbox
▾
Allergen-safeIron-rich ★Hidden vegLow-carbAdult spice
Allergen-safe: Naturally free of egg & nuts. No peas — kidney + black beans. Cheese is melted on top = cooked dairy (no raw dairy).

Ingredients

  • 700 g lean ground beef iron
  • 400 g can kidney beans + 400 g can black beans, drained & rinsed iron + fiber ★
  • 1 onion (~150 g), diced · 2 carrots (~150 g), grated · 1 zucchini (~150 g), grated hidden veg
  • 60 g baby spinach, finely chopped iron
  • 1 bell pepper (~150 g), diced vit C
  • 400 g can diced tomatoes + 30 g tomato paste + 200 g mild salsa vit C
  • 1 tbsp mild chili powder, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp smoked paprika, ½ tsp garlic powder
  • 80 g shredded cheddar (melted on top) · Adults: cayenne / hot sauce / jalapeños

Method

  1. Brown beef with onion. Stir in carrot, zucchini, bell pepper, spinach; cook 4 min.
  2. Add beans, tomatoes, paste, salsa and spices. Simmer 25 min until thick. Keep the pot mild.
  3. Ladle into bowls; top with a little cheddar so it melts in.
🔄 Low-carb: chili is already low-GI (beans + veg). Serve over cauliflower rice instead of rice or bread; skip any cornbread.
🩸 Iron-rich ★: beef + two beans + spinach — the week's iron opener. Bell pepper & salsa add vitamin C; keep the cheese light here.
🌶️ Adults: pull the plain kid portions first, then stir cayenne / hot sauce / jalapeños into the adult bowls.
🥡 Lunchbox: reheats perfectly — microwave 60–90 s. A little cheddar on top for the kids.

Chicken Broccoli Rice Casserole
Monday dinner → Tuesday lunch · serves 4 + lunchbox
▾
Allergen-safeHidden vegIron boostLow-carb swapAdult spice
Allergen-safe: Use a roux (30 g butter + 30 g flour + 400 ml milk) or check the cream-soup can is egg-free. Milk cooks in & cheese melts = cooked dairy. No egg, no nuts. No peas — use corn/green beans.

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken breast or thigh, diced protein
  • 200 g rice (dry), cooked · or cauliflower rice for the low-carb portion
  • 300 g broccoli, finely chopped + 2 carrots (~150 g), grated + 60 g spinach hidden veg + iron
  • 150 g corn no peas
  • Sauce: 30 g butter + 30 g flour + 400 ml milk (roux) or 300 g can cream of chicken/mushroom soup
  • 1 onion (~150 g), 2 garlic cloves · 120 g shredded cheddar
  • Salt, pepper, ½ tsp garlic powder, ½ tsp paprika · Adults: red pepper flakes / hot sauce

Method

  1. Sear diced chicken with onion + garlic until cooked. Cook rice; steam broccoli 3 min.
  2. Make the roux: melt butter, whisk in flour, add milk, simmer to a smooth sauce; stir in half the cheddar, carrot, spinach, corn.
  3. Fold chicken, rice & broccoli through the sauce; spread in a dish, top with cheddar. Bake 190°C (375°F) 25 min.
🔄 Low-carb: build a portion with cauliflower rice instead of rice — the chicken, sauce & broccoli stay; it bakes just the same.
🩸 Iron boost: lighter-iron night — the chopped spinach lifts it. Pair with orange segments or tomato for vitamin C.
🥦 Hidden veg: finely chopped broccoli, grated carrot & spinach disappear into the cheesy sauce.
🌶️ Adults: red pepper flakes or hot sauce on top after the plain kid scoops are out.
🥡 Lunchbox: reheats well — add a splash of milk so it loosens. Microwave 60–90 s.

Beef & Veg Lasagna
Tuesday dinner → Wednesday lunch · serves 4 + lunchbox
▾
Allergen-safeIron-richHidden vegLow-carb swapAdult spice
Allergen-safe: Use egg-free dried lasagna sheets (most dried sheets are semolina + water — check the label). Make the ricotta layer without egg (skip the traditional egg binder); it bakes = cooked dairy. Mozzarella & parmesan melt in. No nuts, no peas.

Ingredients

  • 570 g ground beef iron
  • ~250 g egg-free dried lasagna sheets check label
  • 400 g can diced tomatoes + 400 g passata/marinara + 30 g tomato paste vit C
  • 1 onion (~150 g), 2 garlic cloves · 2 carrots (~150 g) grated · 1 zucchini (~150 g) grated · 60 g spinach hidden veg + iron
  • Ricotta layer: 400 g ricotta + 60 g grated parmesan (no egg)
  • 200 g shredded mozzarella · 1 tsp dried Italian herbs, salt, pepper
  • Adults: red pepper / chili flakes

Method

  1. Brown beef + onion + garlic. Add carrot, zucchini, spinach, tomatoes, passata, paste, herbs; simmer 20 min.
  2. Stir parmesan through the ricotta (no egg). Layer: sauce → sheets → ricotta → mozzarella; repeat; finish with sauce + mozzarella.
  3. Cover, bake 190°C (375°F) 30 min; uncover 10 min more. Rest 10 min before cutting.
🔄 Low-carb: build a portion with thin grilled zucchini or eggplant slices in place of pasta sheets — a "zucchini lasagna"; or use lentil/black-bean sheets.
🩸 Iron-rich: beef + spinach; the tomato base is vitamin C to aid absorption.
🌶️ Adults: stir chili flakes into one half of the sauce before layering, or scatter on top after plating the plain pieces.
🥡 Lunchbox: a firm square reheats cleanly — microwave 60–90 s.

Chicken Quesadillas + Raw Veg
Wednesday dinner → Thursday lunch · serves 4 + lunchbox
▾
Allergen-safeIron-richHidden vegLow-carb swapAdult spicePlain option
Allergen-safe: Check tortillas are egg-free (most flour/corn tortillas are — read the label). Cheese melts = cooked dairy. Keep any sour cream off the allergen-safe portion (raw dairy) — serve it on the side. No egg, no nuts, no peas.

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken breast, cooked & shredded protein
  • 8 tortillas (flour or corn, egg-free) check label
  • 400 g can black beans, drained & rinsed iron + fiber
  • 1 bell pepper diced · 1 zucchini (~150 g) grated · 1 carrot grated · 60 g spinach, finely chopped hidden veg
  • 150 g corn · 200 g shredded Mexican-blend cheese
  • 1 tbsp mild taco seasoning, 1 tsp cumin
  • Raw veg side: carrot, cucumber & bell-pepper sticks vit C
  • On the side: mild salsa · Adults: jalapeños / hot sauce · optional sour cream (adults only)

Method

  1. Warm chicken with beans, corn, bell pepper, grated zucchini/carrot, spinach and mild taco seasoning until the veg softens and liquid cooks off.
  2. Fill tortillas with the mixture + cheese; griddle 2–3 min per side until golden and the cheese melts. Cut into wedges.
  3. Serve with raw veg sticks; keep salsa / sour cream / jalapeños on the side.
🔄 Low-carb: use a low-carb tortilla, or skip the tortilla and serve the filling as a quesadilla bowl over lettuce with cheese melted on top.
🩸 Iron-rich: black beans + spinach; bell pepper & salsa bring the vitamin C.
🌶️ Adults: pull the plain cheese-and-chicken quesadillas for the kids first, then add jalapeños / hot sauce to the adult ones.
🥄 Plain option: a plain chicken-cheese quesadilla, no sauce. 🥡 Lunchbox: wedges pack well with the raw veg sticks; reheat 30–45 s or eat at room temp.

Ham Perogies + Sautéed Veg
Thursday dinner → Friday lunch · serves 4 + lunchbox
▾
Allergen-safeHidden vegIron boostLow-carb swapAdult spice
Allergen-safe: ⚠️ Many store perogies have egg in the dough — buy an egg-free brand or make a simple dough (300 g flour + 150 ml warm water + 15 ml oil + pinch salt). Potato-&-cheese filling is cooked dairy. No nuts, no peas.

Ingredients

  • ~24–30 potato-&-cheese perogies (egg-free) check label
  • 250 g cooked ham, diced
  • 1 onion, sliced · 150 g cabbage, shredded · 2 carrots, grated hidden veg + fiber
  • 60 g baby spinach iron
  • Veg side: bell-pepper & cucumber sticks vit C
  • 15 g butter or oil, salt, pepper · Adults: smoked paprika / chili / hot sauce

Method

  1. Boil perogies 3–4 min until they float, then pan-fry in butter until golden.
  2. Sauté onion, cabbage & carrot until soft; stir in ham to warm, then wilt in the spinach.
  3. Combine perogies with the ham-and-veg; serve with the raw veg sticks.
🔄 Low-carb: perogies are high-GI (potato + dough). Serve the low-carb plate as the ham + buttery sautéed cabbage & onion skillet (2–3 perogies max, or none).
🩸 Iron boost: the lighter-iron night — the wilted spinach helps; pair with vitamin-C bell pepper. The next dinner (bibimbap) is the iron-heavy close.
🌶️ Adults: smoked paprika / chili / hot sauce on the adult skillet; keep the kids' perogies plain and buttery.
🥡 Lunchbox: perogies reheat well — microwave 60–90 s. Pack with the veg sticks.

Bonus: Beef Bibimbap
Friday dinner (weekend, no lunch to match) · serves 4
▾
Allergen-safeIron-rich ★Loads of vegLow-carb swapAdult spiceDeconstructed
Allergen-safe: Traditionally topped with a fried egg → leave it off the egg-free portion. Everything else is safe. Serve it deconstructed: plain rice + plain beef + separate veg, no sauce.

Ingredients

  • 450 g thin-sliced beef (bulgogi cut) iron ★
  • Marinade: 30 ml soy sauce, 15 ml sesame oil, 2 garlic cloves, ¼ pear (grated), 1 tsp sugar
  • Veg: 100 g spinach, 1 carrot, 1 zucchini, 100 g mushrooms, 100 g bean sprouts — each lightly sautéed iron + fiber
  • Rice for the family · eggs for the standard bowls (leave off the egg-free portion)
  • Gochujang sauce on the side (adults + spice to taste)

Method

  1. Marinate beef 20 min, then sear hot & fast. Sauté each veg lightly with a pinch of salt + sesame.
  2. Build bowls: rice, beef, veg. Add fried egg + gochujang to the standard bowls. Plain plate: plain rice, plain beef, veg — no sauce, no egg.
🔄 Low-carb: swap white rice for cauliflower rice or a small scoop of quinoa; pile on veg + beef.
🩸 Iron-rich ★: beef + spinach + vitamin-C veg — a great iron night to close the week.
🥄 Plain option: keep it separate — plain protein + rice, veg on the side.
