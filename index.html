Cocktail Sausage & Pepper Stir-Fry
Sunday dinner → Monday lunch · 2 kid dinners + 2 kid lunches
▾
Allergen-safe (check sausages)Iron-richHidden vegKid portions
Allergen-safe: Check the cocktail sausages — most are just meat and seasoning, but some use egg or milk powder as a binder. The cheese goes on hot, so it's melted.

Ingredients

  • 200 g cocktail sausages, halved iron
  • 1 bell pepper, diced · ½ onion, diced vit C
  • Half a 400 g can black beans, drained & rinsed (~120 g) iron + fiber
  • 1 tbsp ketchup + 1 tsp soy sauce + 2 tbsp water, for a light glaze
  • 1 tsp oil
  • 40 g shredded cheddar, to finish

Method

  1. Brown the sausages in the oil over medium-high heat, 3–4 min.
  2. Add the onion and pepper and stir-fry until just soft, about 4 min.
  3. Tip in the beans, then the ketchup, soy and water. Toss until glossy and heated through.
  4. Scatter the cheddar over each portion so it melts in.
🩸 Iron-rich: sausage plus beans, with the bell pepper bringing plenty of vitamin C.
🥡 Lunchbox: stands in for Monday's daycare tray (Weiner & Beans, Shredded Cheese, Veggies, Fruit, Milk). The other half-can of beans goes into Tuesday's Mexican chicken.

Chicken & Cauliflower Mac and Cheese
Monday dinner → Tuesday lunch · 2 kid dinners + 2 kid lunches
▾
Allergen-safeHidden vegIron boostKid portions
Allergen-safe: Check the macaroni is egg-free — most dried pasta is. The cheese sauce is cooked milk.

Ingredients

  • 170 g chicken thigh, diced
  • 90 g macaroni, egg-free · 120 g cauliflower florets hidden veg
  • Sauce: 10 g butter + 10 g flour + 180 ml milk + 50 g shredded cheddar
  • A handful of baby spinach, finely chopped (optional) iron
  • Salt, pinch of paprika

Method

  1. Boil the macaroni and cauliflower together until tender. Keep a few florets whole.
  2. Sear the chicken until cooked through.
  3. Make the sauce: melt the butter, stir in the flour, whisk in the milk and simmer until thick, then melt in the cheddar. Mash the soft cauliflower into the sauce.
  4. Fold the pasta, chicken, spinach and whole florets through the sauce.
🥦 Hidden veg: cauliflower mashed right into the cheese sauce.
🥡 Lunchbox: matches Tuesday's daycare tray (Chicken, cheese, Cauliflower Macaroni, Fruit, Milk).

Mexican Chicken and Rice
Tuesday dinner → Wednesday lunch · 2 kid dinners + 2 kid lunches
▾
Allergen-safeIron-richHidden vegKid portions
Allergen-safe: Naturally egg-free. Check the taco seasoning is nut-free. Cheese on top is melted.

Ingredients

  • 200 g chicken thigh, diced
  • ½ onion, diced · ½ bell pepper, diced hidden veg
  • 70 g rice (dry)
  • ⅓ of a 400 g can diced tomatoes (~130 g) vit C
  • The other half-can of black beans (~120 g) + 70 g corn iron + fiber
  • ½ tsp mild taco seasoning
  • 200 ml chicken broth
  • 30 g shredded cheddar, to top

Method

  1. Sear the chicken with the onion and pepper.
  2. Stir in the rice, tomatoes, beans, corn, seasoning and broth. Cover and simmer about 20 min until the rice is tender, adding a splash of water if it dries out.
  3. Top with cheddar to melt.
🩸 Iron-rich: chicken and black beans, with tomato for the vitamin C.
🥡 Lunchbox: matches Wednesday's daycare tray (Mexican Chicken and Rice, Fruit, Milk).

Cheeseburger Casserole
Wednesday dinner → Thursday lunch · 2 kid dinners + 2 kid lunches
▾
Allergen-safeIron-richHidden vegKid portions
Allergen-safe: Naturally egg-free — check the pasta label. The cheese is baked on top.

Ingredients

  • 230 g ground beef iron
  • ½ onion, diced · 1 small carrot, grated · ½ zucchini, grated hidden veg
  • 90 g macaroni, egg-free
  • ⅓ of a 400 g can diced tomatoes (~130 g) + 10 g tomato paste vit C
  • 50 g shredded cheddar
  • Salt, pepper, pinch of garlic powder

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 190°C (375°F). Cook the macaroni.
  2. Brown the beef with the onion, then stir in the carrot and zucchini.
  3. Add the tomatoes, paste and cooked pasta; season and mix well.
  4. Spread in a small baking dish, top with cheddar, and bake 15–20 min until bubbling.
🩸 Iron-rich: beef, with tomato for the vitamin C.
🥡 Lunchbox: matches Thursday's daycare tray (Cheeseburger Casserole, Veggies, Fruit, Milk) — add celery sticks.

Allergen-safe (check label)
Allergen-safe: Standard marshmallows are usually egg- and nut-free — check the bag for a nut warning.

Cinnamon Buns
Thursday snack · makes 12, freeze the extras
▾
Allergen-safe (egg swap)
Allergen-safe: Enriched dough usually uses egg — this batch uses a flax egg instead (10 g ground flaxseed + 45 ml water, rested 5 min).

Ingredients

  • 500 g bread flour, 7 g instant yeast, 50 g sugar, ½ tsp salt
  • 250 ml warm milk, 50 g melted butter
  • 1 flax egg (10 g ground flaxseed + 45 ml water, rested 5 min)
  • Filling: 80 g brown sugar, 2 tsp cinnamon, 40 g softened butter
  • Icing (optional): 100 g powdered sugar + 20 ml milk
  • Fresh fruit and milk, to serve

Method

  1. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar and salt. Add the warm milk, melted butter and flax egg; knead into a soft dough. Rise 1 hr.
  2. Roll out, spread with the softened butter, and sprinkle with cinnamon sugar. Roll up and slice into 12.
  3. Rise 30 min more, then bake 180°C (350°F) 20–25 min until golden. Drizzle with icing if using.
  4. Freeze the ones you won't need this week — they reheat well.
🍪 Snack: matches Thursday's daycare snack (Cinnamon Buns, Fruit, Milk).

Dairy-Free Frozen Yogurt Pops
Friday snack · makes ~6 pops
▾
Allergen-safe (dairy swap)
Allergen-safe: Frozen yogurt is still uncooked dairy, so this uses dairy-free coconut or oat yogurt — safe for both kids with no separate batch.

Ingredients

  • 300 g dairy-free coconut or oat yogurt
  • 120 g berries, blended
  • Popsicle molds, or small cups and wooden sticks
  • Fresh fruit and water, to serve

Method

  1. Swirl the blended berries through the yogurt.
  2. Pour into molds and freeze at least 4 hrs. They keep all week, so make them early.
  3. Pack frozen, with fruit and water.
🍪 Snack: matches Friday's daycare snack (Frozen Yogurt Pop, Fruit, Water).
